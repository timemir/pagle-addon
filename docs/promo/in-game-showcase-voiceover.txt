Bind one Fishing key, and every press does the next sensible thing: your pole, a lure, the cast, the reel. One press, one action. Pagle never fishes for you. Now... let's see what's biting.

Well now. That one's worth writing down. Pagle keeps your longest of every fish, so even an ordinary catch can be a personal best.

And that's just the first page. Your Fish Journal remembers every water you've fished. Each zone climbs from Visitor to Master by the different fish you've caught there, not the sheer number. Every fish gets its own page: where it bites, your longest, and up to three stars. Follow it back to its waters... or just search for an old favourite.

No two quite alike, either. Each fish comes in ten colours, up to a single epic. Land all ten, and its crest is yours.

Every catch grows your Angler Level, with one Ledger for all your characters. Discoveries, records, Hunts and goals all add to it, and you can see exactly where it came from. And you've earned a name for yourself, too.

Some catches deserve their own shelf. The Trophy Room keeps the real weights of your biggest fish. The Junk Museum takes everything else you drag up. And Legends: the ones you'll still be talking about long after the bait's gone.

Wondering how the fishing's been? Stats knows every water you've fished, and what bites there. Your odds come only from your own casts, and always say how many they rest on. Watch a fish, and its dry spell follows you on the tracker. Look back over a Session, or a lifetime. Mind you... the next cast still doesn't owe you anything.

Fancy a change of scenery? Hunts are the catches worth planning for: a fish that only bites at night, in one season, or during an event. The board shows what's biting now, what opens soon, and what comes later. Pin one, and it follows you around, with a quiet nod when its window opens. Finish a whole set for its title.

And every few days, Pagle Wagers put a fresh run of Hunts on the table, around a zone or two. Finish them before the run closes; every run you complete counts toward a title.

Or take a smaller adventure from the Tackle Board: three Notices a day, and a longer Expedition each week. They never expire, so a missed day banks instead of vanishing. And if one doesn't suit you, swap it once. Every goal you finish earns Coins, and ties a new fly for your Fly Box. And it keeps your Campfire burning, with a little Kindling for the days life gets in the way.

Those Pagle Coins? You earn them for things you've done, never for sheer grinding, and they only ever buy looks. Toast styles, sound sets, a new binding for your books. Try it on, sign the bill... and it's yours for good. Make Pagle your own.

Master a zone, and its crest is yours to wear on the Journal's bookplate. Fifty-three of them, if you're the patient sort.

And when you'd rather just listen to the water, settle into Cozy mode. Nothing pops up, nothing plays. Everything you earned waits in the Session summary, ready when the fishing's done.

Caught something Pagle doesn't know yet? Share a Field report, and help chart the waters for the next angler.
