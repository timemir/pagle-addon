Part of [Core](README.md). Core presents every Announcement (ADR 0007): Modules raise **moments**, and Core merges, paces and draws them.

## Purpose and boundary

**Owns:** the moment API, the rung ladder, the catch toast, merging a Cast's moments into its Announcements, the display queue, the Interruption budget, the combat hold, Cozy mode, expiry, the toast, Nod, banner and splash frames, the record ruler, Announcement sounds and sound sets, toast styles, the Session summary, the Write-up card, from 1.5.2 the party toasts and cheers (ANN-R103–R109, ANN-R112), and from 1.5.3 the party letter that turns them on (ANN-R113–R116).

**Doesn't own:** deciding what is worth a moment or which rung it gets (each Module), QoL's Nudges (QoL draws them itself; they aren't Announcements), or any Module's own bar, tracker or view.

## Rules

### Moments and the ladder

- **ANN-R1** A Module raises a moment with `M:RaiseMoment(moment)` (fields under [Contracts](#contracts)). Each moment sits on one rung of a fixed ladder, lowest to highest: `nod`, `toast.low`, `toast`, `toast.high`, `banner`, `splash`.
- **ANN-R2** Moments are ranked by rung; on the same rung by the moment's `order` (lower first, default 100); then by the raising Module's `def.order`; then the earliest raised wins.
- **ANN-R3** Moments are display data. Core never tells one Module about another's moments (ADR 0001).
- **ANN-R4** Moments may be raised only while handling a `"live"` entry (LOG-R73), from a Core event handler, or from a Module's own timer or UI. Core drops a moment raised while it is applying a `"replay"` or `"rebuild"` entry, and reports it once per Module per login through `geterrorhandler()`. In a projection's `done`, only `summaryOnly` moments and Write-ups are allowed (LOG-R76); others are dropped the same way.

### A Cast's Announcements

- **ANN-R10** Moments with a `cast` (a Cast's entry ID, or *from 1.6* a Party Catch's, ANN-R110) are merged by that ID. Core collects them and flushes each Cast's moments together on the next frame after the last one arrived, but while any projection is updating (LOG-R74) it holds every Cast's moments and flushes them, Cast by Cast in Cast order, on the frame after the last projection finishes. Live entries reach every projection and the Cast events in one frame (LOG-R9), so a Cast's moments merge whatever order the Modules raised them in.
- **ANN-R11** A Cast's moments become at most **two Announcements**, split by lane (ANN-R20):
  - **big:** its `banner` and `splash` moments
  - **catch:** its `toast.*` and `nod` moments

  A Cast with moments on one side only makes one Announcement. Core hands the big one to the display first, so the two appear together when their lanes are free (a level-up banner and a "New Species" toast). In each, the **headline** is its highest-ranked moment (ANN-R2). Every other moment of that side rides on it as a **stamp** in the corner: the moment's `stamp`, or its `title` if it has none. At most 4 stamps show, highest ranked first; identical stamp texts merge into one; any beyond 4 show as "+N". The Coins stamp and the Refresh stamp (ANN-R23) are separate and never count toward the 4. *From 1.2.3 (#582):* on a toast, the stamps and their "+N" take **three plates** at most: three stamps show as they are; with four or more, the two ranked highest show (in the order above, a variety's own stamp first) and the "+N" counts the rest, those beyond 4 among them ("New variety", "New here", "+2"). Three plates hang as three stamps always did, in at most two rows (UI › Toast): on the Torn Page and Ink and Koi, enUS and deDE, at 10 or 11 with the name line keeping about 95–170 units, where four hung at the last resort's 8 or 9 and left it about 51–63 units, so a name scrolled almost at once (deDE "Öliges S..."). A ruled toast's New record stamp (ANN-R22) and the Coins and Refresh stamps stay outside the three, as they stay outside the 4. A Nod, a banner and a splash still show 4 and "+N". **One exception, a variety's own stamp (#422):** a headline whose moment carries `ownStamp` (a first-catch Common, Uncommon or Rare variety's, VAR-R25) shows its own stamp first, its `stamp` or its `title` as any stamp's, then the others riding on it; it counts among the 4 (a toast's three plates from 1.2.3) and merges with an identical text as any stamp does. The Ledger sets `ownStamp` on those moments only (LED-R97): no other headline shows its own stamp, a New Species and a size record among them, and a variety riding on another headline is a stamp as before. A Nod, which a stepped-down `toast.low` becomes (ANN-R31), has no own stamp. The Announcement's weight is the headline's rung:
  - `splash`: a full-screen splash (a Legend), carrying the Cast's other banner and splash moments as stamps
  - `banner`: a centre banner (level-up, Zone Master, Prestige ready, Prestige, Catch Milestone, set titles, Tackle Board milestones), carrying the Cast's other banners as stamps
  - `toast.*`: a toast above the action bar (Core's catch toast, ANN-R13, is one)
  - `nod`: one silent line
- **ANN-R12** A moment without a `cast` (a Skill Milestone from a Skill Reading, a Hunt window opening, a login Nod) is an Announcement of its own, flushed on the next frame.
- **ANN-R13** An ordinary Catch gets a **catch toast** ([#437](https://github.com/timemir/pagle-addon/issues/437); a silent one-line catch Nod before): Core raises it for every live caught Cast (LOG-R9), while the "Show every catch" setting is on (the default). It is a `toast.low` moment of the Cast (`cast` = its entry ID, `kind` `catch`), raised by Core itself at `CastEnded("caught", cast)`, so it merges with the Cast's other moments (ANN-R10):
  - **Its words** name one of the Cast's Catches: the first, in slot order, with a Simulated Size (LOG-R65), or else the first that isn't item `0`. Its title, the kicker, reads "{band} · {length}": the band's name (`Pagle.SIZE_BANDS`) and the Simulated Size in the Angler's length unit (`Pagle.FormatLength`, LOG-R69), as "DECENT · 34.2 CM" in the toast's capitals; its text is the item's name, as "Mud Snapper". An item with no Simulated Size (junk, treasure, a trophy, an Uncharted item) has the kicker "Caught" over its name. The item's icon is its icon. A name the client hasn't cached reads "Item {itemID}". A Catch with a variety caught before this Cast has it on the text line after the item's name, "Firefin Snapper · ◆ Coral", with its colours ([varieties.md](varieties.md), VAR-R26, VAR-R28): the ring tinted in the rarity's colour, the gem and, for a Rare or Epic one, the gleam, but the item's icon stays in the medallion (the variety's art fills a medallion only on a variety's own first-catch toast, VAR-R25), and no "New variety" stamp; a variety caught for the first time is never named here (VAR-R27). It is drawn full size on the `toast` page like every toast (ANN-R80, UI): its rung only ranks it and sets its hold and budget.
  - **Stepped down.** When the budget steps it down to a Nod (ANN-R31), the Nod reads as the catch Nod did: "{item name} · {band} · {length}", as "Mud Snapper · Decent · 34.2 cm", or the name alone for an item with no size; with a variety caught before, "{item name} · {variety name} · {band} · {length}", with the variety's gem, ring and gleam and the item's icon, as the toast has them (VAR-R26).
  - **A bigger moment replaces it.** If any Module raises a moment for the same Cast, on either side (ANN-R11), the catch toast is dropped: it never headlines, never rides as a stamp and never adds to "+N".
  - **It interrupts as any `toast.low` does.** It counts toward the Interruption budget (ANN-R30, ANN-R31), takes its turn in the column as a toast does (ANN-R20), plays the toast sound and its beats (ANN-R70, ANN-R76), and is held like any toast in combat and while the Field guide is open (ANN-R35–R38). It never goes to the Session summary (an exception to ANN-R25). In Cozy mode it goes nowhere, held or shown (ANN-R40).
  - **Only live Catches.** A got-away Cast, and a Cast whose every item is item `0`, raise none; nor does a replay or rebuild, which fires no `CastEnded` (ANN-R4, LOG-R73).

  With "Show every catch" off, an ordinary Catch raises nothing and never pops up. Either way a Module may move its own bar or view, and a Module raises a moment only for what is worth one: an ordinary Catch is the catch toast's. A moment with no Cast stays what it is: a Hunt window opening, the Pagle Wagers companion hint, Kindling burned and a login Nod are Nods still.

### Party Catches and the party

*From 1.6 (pagle-2026j).* A groupmate's Catch can count toward the Angler's Hunts (LOG-R110–R121, HNT-R180–R187). Two moments come of it: the Angler's own, for what the groupmate's Catch reached, and the **completed for the party** party toast, which shows the party what one Catch did for them. Party toasts are [#640](https://github.com/timemir/pagle-addon/issues/640)'s: groupmates' moments in a small, compact style in the player's Toast column, live only, sent over the party channel (LOG-R110). This section assumes #640 as its issue states it, since its branch has no spec yet: one compact party style, the Toast column's lanes, budget, combat hold, Cozy mode and expiry, a master switch and one switch per kind, and messages of the form `<kind>|<v>|…` on chat type `PARTY` and, as [#644](https://github.com/timemir/pagle-addon/issues/644) decided, their own prefix `PagleCatch`, through #640's send queue (LOG-R110, LOG-R112).

- **ANN-R110** **The Angler's own moments for a Party Catch.** Hunts raises them as for a Cast (HNT-R185), with `cast` set to the Party Catch's entry ID, so Core merges one Party Catch's moments into at most two Announcements as it does a Cast's (ANN-R10, ANN-R11), under every rule a Cast's moments follow (the budget, the combat hold, Cozy mode, the Session summary). Their text names the groupmate as they show ("Night Run · with Kukli Emnabi"), and only then: **no groupmate's name or GUID is ever stored** (the dev's standing decision, 2026-11-08). Each such moment also carries `storedText`, its text with "a groupmate" in place of the name ("Night Run · with a groupmate"), and every copy Core keeps (the Session summary, `global.pending`, the cards, anything in `PagleDB` or `PagleLog`) holds that in place of `text` (announcements.md › Data); `partyCatch`, `partyHunt` and `partyEvent` are never stored. Nothing Pagle sends outside the game names a groupmate either. A Party Catch raises no catch toast (ANN-R13): it isn't the Angler's Catch.
- **ANN-R111** **Completed for the party.** When a live moment that carries `partyCatch` (HNT-R185) is raised and shown (the collector's `show`, so never a replay's, a rebuild's or a `summaryOnly` one, ANN-R4, ANN-R63), Core sends a party toast of kind `hp` ("Hunt for the party"), version 1, to the party on the party channel, on the prefix `PagleCatch` like every `<kind>|<v>|` message (LOG-R110), through its send queue (LOG-R112) but not ahead of it (only a party catch message goes first), whether or not it pops up for the player (Cozy mode included), while the Party Hunts setting (LOG-R121; off until the party letter is accepted, ANN-R113) and **Share my moments with my party** (SET-R36) are on, in a party (LOG-R110). No answered hello is needed: a Party Catch already shows the groupmate runs Pagle. The message is `hp|1|<what>|<id>|<sourceCatchId>`: `<what>` is `h` for a Standing Hunt completed, its Hunt ID as `<id>` (the moment's `partyHunt`), or `w` for a settled run, its event ID as `<id>` (the moment's `partyEvent`); `<id>` is the ID as it is, 1 to 64 of `a-z`, `0-9` and `_`; and `<sourceCatchId>` is the groupmate's Catch ID the moment carries, `Player-<realm>-<ID>-<time>-<tie>:<n>`. A message past the addon message limit (255 bytes) isn't sent. It names no zone and no player: the receiving clients know who sent it.
  - **Who sees it.** A receiver reads `hp` on the prefix `PagleCatch` and the party channel, checking the sender as LOG-R113 items 1, 2 and 4 do (not the player; Party Hunts on, in a party; one of the party units, matched as ANN-R105 matches a groupmate), then that it is exactly those five fields, version `1` (a later version is ignored, LOG-R110), and that the sender is within their own limit: 4 at once, then one per 10 s (Tunables), since one Party Catch can complete Hunts and settle a run at once. Anything else is ignored without a word. It shows for every groupmate whose party toasts are on (Show my party's moments, ANN-R106) and whose "Completed for the party" kind switch is on (SET-R35, SET-R36). The catcher, whose own GUID opens `<sourceCatchId>`, sees "Your catch" as the kicker; every other groupmate sees "Completed for the party".
  - **What it shows.** One compact party toast, the party style (ANN-R107), its title the kicker above, its text "{Hunt name} · for {sender}" or, for a run, "{event name} settled · for {sender}", each groupmate named by their first name. Names come from the receiver's own lore (`hunt.<id>.name`, `event.<id>.name`, HNT-R130), through Hunts' def (`partyHuntName`, `partyEventName`, Contracts); an event the receiver doesn't know (a pushed one it never logged) reads "a Pagle Wager", and a Hunt it doesn't know, or any with Hunts not On, "a Hunt". The icon is Hunts' `Trade_Fishing`. It has no mug, so the words take the mug's room, and the line shrinks to 0.6 of its size (the other party toasts' 0.7) before it is cut: a run or a Hunt for one or two groupmates fits in every language. Stepped down to a Nod (ANN-R31) it reads "Completed for the party", then its line.
  - **Merging.** `hp` toasts with the same `<sourceCatchId>` and `<id>` that arrive within the party merge window (Tunables) of the first become one toast, listing every sender once, in the order they came: "Night Run · for Kukli and Bram", "· for Kukli, Bram and 2 more". So a Catch that completes a Hunt for the whole party shows once on each screen.
  - It is a `toast.low` in rank and budget, like any party toast, and plays the party arrive sound as one does (ANN-R108); it carries no Coins, no Refreshes and no cheers button, never reaches the Session summary, and is dropped, not held, if it waits longer than #640's expiry, counted from its first message. Nothing of it is logged.

### The display queue

- **ANN-R20** Core shows Announcements in two places: a banner or splash in the **centre**, one at a time, and toasts and Nods in one **column**, the **Toast column**, at the bottom centre unless the Angler moved it (ANN-R98). A banner and the column show together. A splash is full-screen, so it holds the column back: it waits until no toast is in the column, and while it waits or shows, nothing new enters the column.

  | Place | Shows | Stays | Waiting |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Column | `toast.*` headlines as toasts, `nod` headlines (and `toast.low` headlines stepped down, ANN-R31, among them the catch toast, ANN-R13) as Nods | until its motion settles (a toast 2.7 s, a Nod 0.3 s), then its hold: every toast 6 s, whatever its rung (`toast.low`, the catch toast and a variety's own toast, VAR-R28, included; `toast.high` too) and ruled or not, as every toast is drawn full size (ANN-R80, #468); "While you were fighting" 8 s; a Nod 3 s | first in first out, unlimited; the next enters when the column has room: at most 4 entries, 3 of them toasts, and 300 units tall, so it never reaches a banner |
  | Centre | `banner` and `splash` headlines | a banner settles (1.4 s), then stays 4.5 s; a splash until clicked or 8 s | unlimited, splashes before banners, otherwise in order raised; the next starts once the last has left (a banner takes 0.5 s to leave) |

  The newest entry enters at the column's foot and the older ones move up (0.3 s). With the pointer on a toast or Nod, its clock stands still, and clicking one sends it away at once, then runs its headline's `onClick` if it has one (a Module's own action, such as opening its view; an error in it is reported through `geterrorhandler()`). A Shift-drag of one moves the column instead, and neither sends it away nor runs its `onClick` (ANN-R98). *Added while building (#305): the companion Nod opens the Pagle Wagers spread when clicked (HNT-R171), which needed a way for a Module to act on a click.* One leaving fades out drifting up, and the ones above drop into its place (UI › Motion).
- **ANN-R21** **Cards** (the Session summary and the Write-up) show only when no toast, banner or splash is on screen or waits (Nods don't count). They don't count as interruptions and play no sound. At login, held Announcements show first, then the Write-up, then "Your last session".
- **ANN-R98** **The Toast column moves.** The Angler can move the whole column, every toast and Nod in it together; banners and splashes never move.
  1. **Drag.** A Shift-drag with the left button on any toast or Nod moves the column, every entry following the pointer with its stacked offsets (ANN-R20). The column always grows upward from its foot, the newest at the foot. A drag never starts without Shift or while `Pagle.IsRestricted()`, and it never sends the toast away or runs its `onClick`, the mouse-up that ends it included. A plain click, and a Shift-click that didn't drag, do as ANN-R20 says.
  2. **Held while dragged.** From the drag's start to its drop the column is held: every toast's and Nod's clock stops, not only the one under the pointer's, nothing enters the column (what comes waits its turn, first in first out) and nothing in it leaves. At the drop every clock goes on from where it stood and what waited enters. The pointer leaving a toast mid-drag doesn't start its clock again. The centre goes on as ever.
  3. **The foot.** The foot is the column's BOTTOM centre, where the newest entry enters. It is saved per profile in `profile.announcements.point = { x, y }`, in UIParent's units from UIParent's BOTTOM centre, so the UI scale never moves it. With none saved it is `{ x = 0, y = 156 }`, where the column stood before it could move. A saved value that isn't a table with two finite numbers `x` and `y` counts as none.
  4. **Clamp.** The foot is kept where a full column fits the screen: `y` from 0 up to the screen's height less the column's 300 and a toast.high bookmark's 22; `x` no further from the centre than half the screen's width less half of the widest entry, a Nod's 22 + 640 (the widest toast page, 560, is narrower). The dropped foot is clamped and saved so. Each time the column is placed the saved foot is clamped as the screen is then, and the saved value is left as it is, so a screen or UI scale change can't push the column off screen and a bigger screen gives it back. A screen too short for a full column puts the foot at `y` 0, one too narrow at `x` 0.
  5. **Restriction.** A drag in progress when restriction starts stops where it is and is saved, as a drop (the toasts go off screen anyway, ANN-R35).
  6. **Reset.** The Announcements page's "Toast position" button (SET-R34) clears `announcements.point` and puts the column back at the default, the toasts and Nods on screen moving with it, and puts the card back too (ANN-R102). Nothing while restricted. The page's Defaults button leaves the point alone (SET-R60).
  7. **The Letter** shows at the same foot, clamped the same way, wherever the Angler moved it (ONB-R11). Margin notes (ONB-R52) read where the column's frames are when each is placed, so they need nothing.
  8. **The hint.** *From 1.2.1 (#582):* the pointer on a toast or Nod says how the column moves: "Shift-drag to move" (`SHIFT_DRAG_HINT`, until 1.2.4 the card's hint's string too, ANN-R102) in GameTooltip at the entry's right, so it covers no entry above it. Only while no foot is saved (the Angler hasn't moved the column, or pressed Reset since), and never while restricted or during a drag. The pointer leaving, a drag starting and the toast or Nod leaving take it away. The Announcements page's Reset tooltip says it as before (SET-R34).
- **ANN-R22** A moment may carry a **record ruler** (`ruler = { old, new, unit, label }`). On a toast headline, the ruler fills past the old record's mark while the number counts up, shows the "+x" margin, then the "New record" stamp lands. On a Nod headline, a compact ruler with the margin shows instead. A ruler shows only for the headline; a record riding as a stamp shows only its stamp. `label` is the small word at the ruler's foot, at the bottom left of the toast, in plain words, never where it comes from: "weight" for a trophy weight; for a Simulated Size the Catch's Size band ("Tiddler" to "Monster"), which a size record's toast shows there rather than in its title (the Module's own title names the fish alone), and "length" only when Core names no band for it. A ruler's label is never a word of the headline's title or text. For a Simulated Size, `unit` is `"length"` and `old` and `new` are millimetres: Core shows them and the margin with `Pagle.FormatLength` in the Angler's length unit ([catch-log.md](catch-log.md#simulated-size), LOG-R69), and a margin that rounds to zero in that unit shows as "+<0.1 cm" or "+<0.1 in". Any other `unit` is shown as given ("lb"). A Module never leaves the ruler off a size record.
- **ANN-R23** A moment may carry `coins = N`. Core sums the `coins` of the moments in each Announcement into its own "+N" Pagle Coins stamp, always shown (outside the 4-stamp limit and the merging of identical stamps), Nods included. A Cast's two Announcements each sum only their own moments, so no Coins show twice. Coins never make an Announcement of their own, and a purchase makes none ([wallet-and-shop.md](wallet-and-shop.md)).
  A moment may likewise carry `tickets = N`, the Refreshes its event earned ([refresh.md](refresh.md), REF-R7). Core sums the `tickets` of the moments in each Announcement into one "Refresh +N" stamp, always shown and outside the 4-stamp limit, the merging and the "+N" for the rest: the last stamp, after the "+N". A Nod, a banner and the "While you were fighting" toast (which sums the toasts it holds, ANN-R36) carry it as they carry Coins. A moment with no `tickets`, or 0, changes nothing. A Refresh makes no Announcement of its own and no sound, and the Session summary has no Refresh line.
- **ANN-R24** Ordinary display picks the headline's sound (ANN-R71) when the Announcement appears on screen, never when it is queued. When a toast appears in the same frame as a banner or splash that played its sound (a Cast's two Announcements, or the release after combat, ANN-R36), only the higher rung's sound plays: the toast is silent, and the next toast plays the variant this one would have. A toast's beats follow its sound (ANN-R76).
- **ANN-R25** Every moment also goes to the Session summary (ANN-R50), whether or not it popped up. The catch toast is the one exception: it is Core's own, never a Module's moment, and never reaches the summary (ANN-R13).

### Interruption budget

- **ANN-R30** An **interruption** is an Announcement shown as a toast, banner or splash, the catch toast (ANN-R13) included. Each one shown counts on its own: a Cast's banner and its toast are 2. Nods, among them a toast stepped down, and cards aren't interruptions.
- **ANN-R31** With the budget on (the default), when an Announcement with a `toast.low` headline reaches the front of the column's queue, Core counts the interruptions shown in the last 5 minutes. If there are already 40 (the default count, 20 before #437; ONB-R35's Lively preset), it steps down to a Nod (with a compact ruler if it has one) and takes its turn as one. The catch toast (ANN-R13) is such a headline: it counts, and steps down when the budget is spent, to the one line the catch Nod was. Every other rung always gets through and always counts.
- **ANN-R32** With the budget off, nothing steps down. The count and window are player settings.

### Combat hold

- **ANN-R35** While `Pagle.IsRestricted()` is true, Core hides whatever is on screen and holds it, together with everything waiting and every new Announcement and card, sounds included; Nods on screen or waiting are dropped (a catch toast stepped down is one). Announcements raised during the 2 s wait after combat (ANN-R36) are held too.
- **ANN-R36** 2 s after `RestrictionChanged(false)` (restarting the wait if a new restriction starts first), Core shows what it held, dropping expired moments (ANN-R45):
  1. each held splash and banner, as itself, in the centre (splashes first, then in the order raised)
  2. the held toasts, in the column at the same time as the first held banner (a held splash still holds them back, ANN-R20), so a held Cast's big and catch Announcements show together, as they would have live: 1 or 2 as themselves, in order; 3 or more as one "While you were fighting" toast listing every held headline and their Coins. That toast plays the toast sound once unless it appears with a banner (ANN-R24), counts as one interruption, and never steps down.
  3. held cards (ANN-R21), once no toast, banner or splash shows or waits

  Held Nods don't show again; they are already in the Session summary. A catch toast is held as any toast is (ANN-R13), and the digest can't flood with them: nothing is caught while fighting, so at most the one or two Casts landing as combat starts are held.
- **ANN-R37** Held Announcements count toward the budget only when they actually show.
- **ANN-R38** **Hold reasons.** The hold is kept per reason: `combat` (ANN-R35, ANN-R36) and, from M8.5, `guide`, while the Field guide is open (ONB-R70). While any reason holds, everything is held as in ANN-R35, Nods dropped; a catch toast is held with the toasts (ANN-R13). When the last reason ends, what was held shows by the rules of the reason that held it longest: after `combat`, as ANN-R36 says; after `guide` alone, at once, with held toasts shown as themselves in order, never folded into "While you were fighting". A combat hold that starts while the guide holds closes the guide (ONB-R25), so `combat`'s rules apply.

### Cozy mode

### Party toasts and cheers

*From 1.5.2 ([#640](https://github.com/timemir/pagle-addon/issues/640)).* In a party, a groupmate who also runs Pagle and has a moment worth sharing shows in the player's own column as a small **party toast**, with a raised mug to cheer it; the groupmate then sees who raised one. Live only: add-on messages over the party channel, nothing in `PagleLog`, the Catch log, any projection or ranked state, and no backend. A groupmate's moment is shown as their claim, cosmetic only. Minimal by the dev's decision: no party panel or tracker, so a groupmate's Pagle shows only through party toasts and cheers. It ships as a patch, so none of it is in a hashed file: Core's part is in `adapter/` (`adapter/PartyMessages.lua`, pure Lua the specs drive with no client, then `adapter/Party.lua`, `adapter/PartyToast.lua` and `adapter/PartySettings.lua`), loaded by `adapter/Settings.xml`'s `<Script>` lines since the TOC's file list can't change, and the Modules' part is a field their adapters set (ANN-R104). *From 1.6,* party-wide Hunts (ANN-R110, ANN-R111, LOG-R110–R126) share the prefix, the channel and the send queue (LOG-R112), and add their own kind of party toast, "Completed for the party" (ANN-R111).

- **ANN-R103** **The party.** A party is `IsInGroup()` and not `IsInRaid()`: the player and up to four groupmates; a raid has no party toasts and sends nothing. Core registers the add-on message prefix `Pagle` at each load and speaks only on the `PARTY` channel. Every message starts with the protocol version, `1`; Contracts › Party messages has the format. **Hello.** A groupmate is a **Pagle groupmate** once any valid message (ANN-R105) came from them. Core says hello (`1:H:1`) 2 s after the roster last changed when a groupmate joined since its last hello (each new member is greeted once), and at login or a `/reload` in a party; a `1:H:1` from a groupmate is answered with `1:H:0` unless Core said hello in the last 10 s. Leaving the party forgets every Pagle groupmate; a groupmate who leaves is forgotten. **Sending** is paced by Core's own limiter, well under the client's per-prefix allowance (10, then 1 a second): a burst of 3, then one a second, at most 8 waiting, a message past that dropped; nothing is sent while `C_ChatInfo.InChatMessagingLockdown()` (it waits), nor outside a party. Nothing is sent but these three messages. *From 1.6* the same queue and limiter also carry the party catch message, on its own prefix `PagleCatch`, ahead of these (catch-log.md LOG-R110–R112): one queue, the same 3, then one a second, at most 8 waiting. From 1.5.3 the hello goes out whether or not the player has accepted the party letter, and carries no moment and no place (ANN-R113).
- **ANN-R104** **Shared moments.** A Module marks a moment its player may share with `party`, a kind (Contracts): the Ledger a Legend's first Catch (`legend`), a Rare or Epic variety's first Catch (`colour`), a size record in the Monster band (`monster`, its ruler's label is the Monster band's name), a Zone Master (`zone`), a Prestige (`prestige`) and Ledger completion's title (`ledger`, the `completeTitle` moment naming `complete.ledger`'s title, LED-R75); Hunts a Hunt's gold tier (`gold`, the moment of the first tier whose trim is gold, HNT-R86, with the Hunt's ID in `partyHunt`). A Zone Master's moment also carries its zone's areaID in `partyArea`: the Ledger's adapter maps the zone name its moment was written with back to the areaID it looked the name up by. The adapters set these as each moment is raised (Hunts by wrapping `ns.HuntTierMoment`), reading only what the Module already computed, so what the Modules compute and say is unchanged. While **Share my moments with my party** is on (SET-R36; from 1.5.3 off until the party letter is answered, ANN-R113), in a party with at least one Pagle groupmate, Core sends each marked moment it shows (the collector's `show`, so only live ones: never a replay, rebuild or `summaryOnly` moment, ANN-R4, ANN-R63) once, as `1:M`, whether or not it pops up for the player (Cozy mode included). It carries only the moment: the kind, the item, a variety's rarity and place in its Species' ten, a record's length, a Prestige rank, the zone a Zone Master mastered (its areaID) and the Hunt a gold tier was reached in (its ID). Never where the player is: the mastered zone is the achievement, not a position, and nothing else of place is sent. Each sent moment has an id, a 4-character nonce made at load and a counter, both base 36; Core keeps its last 20 ids for 10 minutes, for cheers (ANN-R108).
- **ANN-R105** **Receiving.** A `CHAT_MSG_ADDON` is read only when all of these hold, and is otherwise ignored, silently: the prefix is `Pagle` and the channel `PARTY`; the player is in a party, not a raid; neither the text nor the sender is a secret value (CORE-R31); the sender is a groupmate in `party1`–`party4` and isn't the player (the channel echoes the player's own): matched by GUID, `UnitGUID(sender)` (a groupmate's name is a unit token) against `UnitGUID("partyN")`, or, where the client resolves no GUID, by first name (the sender's before any `-realm` or space, against `UnitName`'s first return) only when exactly one groupmate has it, so two groupmates of one first name are never confused; the text is at most 64 bytes of letters, digits and `:` only, so no escape sequence can reach the screen; its version is `1`; its type and fields are exactly one of Contracts › Party messages, every number in range and the kind known; and the sender is within their **rate limit**, a bucket per groupmate and type: hellos 2, then one per 30 s; moments 3, then one per 20 s; cheers 6, then one per 5 s. A moment id already received from that groupmate is ignored. A valid message marks its sender a Pagle groupmate (ANN-R103). Core keeps groupmates by GUID. **Their name**, on a party toast and a cheer's Nod, is "{name} {surname}" (`PARTY_FULL_NAME`): `UnitName("partyN")`'s first return and, where Forever gives one, its surname, the second (Core reads the player's own the same way, adapter/Angler.lua), as the website writes an Angler (pagle-backend ANG-R13); the first name alone without one.

### Party messages

*From 1.5.2* (ANN-R103–R109). Prefix `Pagle`, channel `PARTY`, plain text: fields joined by `:`, at most 64 bytes, letters, digits and `:` only. Numbers are whole, in base 10, with no sign or leading `+`.

| Message | Fields | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Hello | `1:H:<ask>`: `ask` `1` asks for an answer, `0` is one | `1:H:1` |
| Moment | `1:M:<id>:<kind>[:<args>]`, `id` 4–12 of `0-9a-z` | `1:M:k3x91:legend:19803` |
| Cheer | `1:C:<id>`: the id of the moment cheered | `1:C:k3x91` |

| Kind | Args | Range |
|---|---|---|
| `legend` | item | item 1–999999999 |
| `colour` | item, rarity, place | rarity `r` or `e`; place 0–10 (0: not found) |
| `monster` | item, length in mm | length 1–99999 |
| `gold` | item, Hunt | item 0–999999999 (0: none); the Hunt's ID, 1–32 of `a-z0-9`, each `_` written `Z` (`mackerel_run` as `mackerelZrun`), or `0` for none |
| `zone` | area | the zone's areaID, 0–9999999 (0: none) |
| `prestige` | rank | 1–999 |
| `ledger` | none | |

The longest moment, a 12-character id and a 32-character Hunt, is 64 bytes. *From 1.6* the messages of the form `<kind>|<v>|…` (the party catch message, LOG-R111, and ANN-R111's `hp`) don't use this envelope: they ride their own prefix, `PagleCatch`, each whole in one addon message of at most 255 bytes, through the same send queue (catch-log.md LOG-R110, LOG-R112; decided in [#644](https://github.com/timemir/pagle-addon/issues/644)). A 1.5.2 client never registers that prefix, so it never receives them; one on the prefix `Pagle` would fail this envelope and be ignored (ANN-R105).

`seal` is what a banner headline's wax seal shows: a number pressed into it (an Angler Level, a Catch Milestone's count), a pressed mark (`"waves"`, `"book"`, `"quill"`), `"frame"` for the Prestige frame of `frameRank` holding the Angler's portrait (ANN-R83), or `"crest"` with `crest` set, that icon (a fish crest, VAR-R25) in the seal's place. Any other value, or none (or a `"frame"` without `frameRank`, a `"crest"` without `crest`), shows the waves. Only a banner headline's is read.

`ownStamp` is the exception to "a headline never shows its own stamp" (ANN-R11), only for a first-catch variety's moment (VAR-R28). On a toast headline Core then draws the toast on the `toast` page whatever its rung, its `kicker` (when it has one, else its `title`) as the printed line over the name; the `title` and `text` still name the moment to the Session summary, the Write-up and a Nod, and a moment that rides on another headline ignores all three fields.

`art` is a texture and its rectangle as texture coordinates (a fish variety's art, VAR-R11), drawn only where the moment is the headline: a toast's or Nod's medallion shows it cropped to the fish's head (the square at the art's left, from 3% to 40.5% of its width and 12.5% to 87.5% of its height) in place of `icon`, and a banner shows it whole across its seal's foot, the seal's ribbon. `icon` still names what the moment is about, for the Session summary, which never stores `art`. A moment with `variety` is the exception to "only where the moment is the headline" on a toast: its art fills the medallion of the toast it rides on too, and the toast takes the variety's colours (VAR-R28). `variety` is a name Core may print and a rarity key (`common` to `epic`, VAR-R2); a moment whose rarity Core doesn't know colours nothing.

### `Pagle.Animate(owner, animations, onDone?)`, `Pagle.Cue(owner, seconds, fn)`, `Pagle.StopMotion(owner)`

Core's one motion driver (UI › Motion), which Modules move their frames with instead of a copy of their own:

```lua
Pagle.Animate(frame, {
  { dur = 0.16, ease = Pagle.EASE.out, keys = { { 0, { 0 } }, { 1, { 1 } } }, apply = function(v) frame:SetAlpha(v[1]) end },
  { dur = 1.4, ease = Pagle.Bezier(0.3, 1.5, 0.5, 1), iter = "infinite", dir = "alternate", keys = …, apply = … },
}, onDone)
```

- An animation is the art kits' keyframes: `keys` are `{ progress 0..1, { values } }` as the CSS percentages, a key's `ease` easing the segment after it; `iter` is a count or `"infinite"`, `dir` `"normal"`, `"alternate"` or `"alternate-reverse"`, `delay` in seconds; `apply(values)` sets the property. `Pagle.EASE` holds `linear`, `ease`, `in`, `out` and `inOut`; `Pagle.Bezier` makes a CSS cubic-bezier.
- Each starts at its first frame at once, so nothing shows its settled state first; animations started on an owner already playing play beside what it plays. `onDone` runs once all have finished. `Pagle.Cue` runs `fn` that many seconds on, on the owner's clock (a sound on a beat). `Pagle.StopMotion` stops what the owner plays, where it is, with no `onDone`.
- The driver reads the Animations setting itself: off, each animation jumps to where it settles and `onDone` runs at once, and a cue never runs.
- An owner that is no longer visible while it moves (closed, or the whole UI hidden) settles there: each animation jumps to where it settles (a loop to where it starts), each cue still to come runs, then `onDone`. Nothing it ends with, such as hiding a card or closing a note, is lost. A loop starts again only when its Module starts it, as its frame shows.
- The driver ticks only while something moves (QOL-R25).

### `Pagle.WheelScroll(scroll, delta, options?)`

What a scroll frame's `OnMouseWheel` calls, so every book and list scrolls alike: `delta` notches (the client's +1 a notch up, −1 down) of **a third of the view** each, so two thirds of what showed stay in sight. The step is in the view's own units, so a window `Pagle.FitToScreen` shrinks still moves the same share of what it shows. It glides there on the motion driver (0.12 s, `EASE.out`, the scroll frame its owner); a notch while a glide runs adds to where that glide was going, not to where it has got to, so a quick spin goes as far as its notches, and each glide starts where the last one is, so nothing jumps. It never passes the top or the foot, and gives way to anything else that scrolls the frame while it glides (a page sent back to its top, a dragged scroll bar). With Animations off it jumps there.

```lua

---@field height number?            -- the view's height in the scroll frame's units (default its GetHeight)
---@field range (fun(): number)?    -- how far it scrolls (default its GetVerticalScrollRange)
---@field onScroll fun(offset)?     -- runs at each step of the glide: a scroll bar or thumb that follows it
```

## UI

Every frame is drawn from the announcements art kit: a toast is a fresh entry in the Angler's ledger. The art ships as textures in `addons/Pagle/media/announce/` (BLP, DXT5; `scripts/encode-blp.py` encodes the kit's atlases), with their atlases in `data/AnnounceArt.lua`, and three bundled fonts in `addons/Pagle/media/fonts/`: IM Fell English for print, Caveat for the Angler's hand, Marcellus for gilt, stamps and labels. A headline with a text has its title printed in capitals over the text in the hand; a headline with no text has its title alone in the hand. Stamps, labels and printed words are in capitals (the client has no small caps), leaving texture and colour escapes as they are.

- **Toast column:** toasts and Nods stack in one column, the newest at its foot and the older ones above, 12 units apart. The foot is 156 units above the screen's bottom, centred, until the Angler moves the column (ANN-R98).
- **Toast:** above the action bar, a torn page on a leather spine with the icon in a brass medallion, the stamps right to left from the page's top right corner, and the Coins beside a brass coin at the bottom right; the words end 4 units before the coin and its "+N" as they draw, so a long name is cut before them, never under them. Every toast draws full size (#468): `toast.low` and `toast` alike on the `toast` page (464 × 92, the 84 medallion) on the spine, `toast.high` on a gilt page with a halo, a silk bookmark, a bronze medallion and a wax seal. Its kicker is in the print face at 16 (13 before #467; a composed style's kicker is 16/13 of its kit's). A headline with a record ruler is on the ruled page (`toast.high`'s too, the high page having no room for one): the label (a size record's Size band) at the bottom left, a ticked track filling up to the number, the old record's pencil mark with its number, the margin in red, then the "New record" stamp beside the others. On a toast with no ruler, the name line is laid where the kicker and the name line, as one block, are centred down the page, as the medallion is, by the height the two lines measure once written (not by their fonts' sizes). The kicker is not centred with it: its top is fixed, 10 units down the page (a Torn Page toast's baseline 24.5 down), so that its capitals stand at least 3 units above the page's red ruling line, which the page art draws from 34 units down at the words' left to 28 at the widest a kicker reaches (the other styles' pages have it too), for every name, size band and row of stamps; a composed style's kicker is centred with its name line by their fonts' own sizes, as its kit's art has no such line; on a ruled toast the block stays at the page's top, the name line ends before the ruler's landed number, and the label stands in the toast's bottom left corner, at the words' margin, under the ruler's row. The stamps hang from the top right, squeezed in steps to 0.7 of their size (words and padding together) so that the row, a ruled toast's "New record" stamp with them, ends right of the kicker and a gap; if it still doesn't at 0.7, the kicker's font steps down from 16 toward 13 until it does. *From 1.2.1 (#582):* a stamp's word is never squeezed under 0.9 of its size (the Stamp font's 11 at 10) while anything else can make room. Every stamp, in either row, ends right of the kicker and its gap of 10, and the stamps hang by the first of these that fits: (1) in one row, at full size or 0.9, the kicker's font stepping down a unit at a time from 16 toward 13 (a composed style's from its 16/13 toward its kit's size) between; (2) in two rows, the same way from the kicker's full size: the stamps that don't fit in the first, in order, hang in a **second row** under it, from the same right edge, level, a stamp's height and 4 lower (a composed style's plate's height and 4), a ruled toast's "New record" stamp last of all wherever it falls; (3) only then, at the kicker's floor, in two rows squeezed to 0.8, then 0.7, where they stay, which only a stamp too wide for any row beside the kicker needs. A second row that hangs as low as the Coins stand ends 4 before them, as the words do; the name line's room ends 8 before a second row, so a longer name scrolls in its clip (ANN-R101) instead of running under it (*from 1.3.3,* [#592](https://github.com/timemir/pagle-addon/issues/592), also 8 before the art Ink and Koi and the Town Crier's Proclamation draw over the words where it rests on the line's end: the koi, and the seal on its ribbons; a long name, deDE's "Öliges Schwarzmaul" among them, ended under it, and on a still sample, the Field guide's Moments toast's, it stayed there); and a ruled toast keeps its stamps to one row in a style whose second row would end less than 8 above the ruler's top, where the ruler's numbers stand (the Deep Lure, Fishmonger's Slate). Widow's Web and the Cartographer's Folio keep their stamps to one row on every toast, as their art drawn over the words (the spider on its thread, the compass rose) covers where a second row would hang (#584's review); there a toast's stamps squeeze to 0.8 and 0.7 as the last resort, as before 1.2.1. "While you were fighting" keeps one row. Before 1.2.1 the row squeezed to 0.7 before the kicker stepped down, so two stamps beside a long kicker showed at 8 (#582). On a ruled or taller page the words stay at the top of the page's text box, never moved down with the page's lower half. A toast announcing a variety's first catch (VAR-R28) shows the variety's art on its disc in the medallion in place of the icon, its ring and "New variety" stamp in the rarity's colour, and writes its name after a small gem on its name line; the catch toast naming a variety caught before has the ring, the gem and the gleam, with the item's icon. A first-catch Common, Uncommon or Rare variety that headlines its toast has the default `toast` page, the Catch's size as its kicker (as the catch toast's), the fish's name then the gem and the variety on its line, and its own stamp ("NEW VARIETY" or "RARE VARIETY") first in the stamp row. The catch toast (ANN-R13) is drawn on the `toast` page like any: its kicker is the band and length, or "Caught", and its name line the fish's name, with a variety caught before written as VAR-R28 says, ring, gem and gleam but no art and no stamp. The pointer on it stops its clock, and a click sends it away (ANN-R20).
- **Nod:** one line in the column on a torn strip sized to it, with the icon in a small medallion: the words, a compact ruler (the track, the number and the margin), the stamps and the Coins. A catch toast stepped down (ANN-R13, ANN-R31) reads its old one line, "{item} · {band} · {length}". One naming a variety (VAR-R26) writes the variety's name in its rarity's ink after a small gem in its colour (a diamond rimmed in the ink), the rest of the line in the style's ink, and shows the variety's art cropped to the fish's head in the medallion, its ring tinted the rarity's colour (Common's stays the style's).
- **Banner:** centre screen over the world with no frame: a dark glow and faint gilt rays (ANN-R99, ANN-R100), the title in spaced gilt capitals, gilt tooling either side of a red wax seal (the headline's `seal`, under Contracts), the text in gilt handwriting, then the stamps as leather labels and the Coins.
- **ANN-R99** **A banner's dark glow has no edge.** The glow is the art's soft ellipse (980 units wide) behind the whole banner, from its top, the kicker's line included, down 340 units, and no side of it is a straight edge: the art fades to nothing at its sides and foot, and its top 130 units are drawn through a gradient from clear at the top to opaque, so it comes in softly instead of being cut off level with the seal. One frame draws every banner, so each seal (a number, a Prestige frame, a crest good's icon, a variety's ribbon), every toast style and the Curio Shop's still sample have the same glow.
- **ANN-R100** **A banner's rays turn.** Its gilt rays turn clockwise about the seal's centre, once in 60 s (the splash's rate), for as long as the banner shows, and stop as it goes; with Animations off (SET-R30) they stand still, upright. Their art is square and fades to nothing inside its inscribed circle, so no edge or corner of it shows however far they have turned. They are not a Cozy mode matter: Cozy mode shows no banner (ANN-R40).
- **ANN-R101** **A long name line scrolls.** A toast's name line (its text under the kicker, with a variety's gem and name after it, ANN-R13, VAR-R28) that is still wider than its room once its font has stepped down as far as it goes is not cut short with "...": it is written whole, every character as it was, and scrolls inside the clip it is written out in, so the Angler can read all of it. Once the name is written out (UI › Motion), the line shows its start for 1 s, glides left at a steady 40 units a second until its end shows, holds there 1 s, glides back to its start at the same speed, and again, for as long as the toast shows. It stops where it is as the toast starts to leave and is back at its start once the toast is gone, so the next toast drawn in that frame starts at its own start; nothing ticks while no line scrolls. If the whole UI is hidden while it scrolls (Alt+Z), the line settles at its start and doesn't scroll again for that toast: shown again, it sits at its start, cut at the clip's edge, as any loop the motion driver settles starts again only when it is started (Contracts › `Pagle.Animate`). Only the line moves: the clip stays where the name was written, 4 units before it, and ends where the line's room does (4 units before the Coins as they draw, ANN-R80; before a ruled toast's landed number; *from 1.2.1,* 8 before a second row of stamps, UI › Toast; *from 1.3.3,* 8 before Ink and Koi's koi and the Town Crier's Proclamation's seal where they rest, UI › Toast), so the line never draws over the page's art, the medallion or the Coins, and the toast's size and layout are as they were. A line that fits never moves. With Animations off (SET-R30), and on a still sample (the Curio Shop's stage), it is cut short as before. In every toast style, composed ones too; a headline's title alone (a toast with no text) and the Nod are as they were.
- **Splash:** full screen for a Legend, until clicked: the screen darkens behind slow rays, the item's icon sits in the well of the Legend's own crest (LED-R153; the Unwritten crest for a Legend with none), its motion playing while the splash holds, and a torn page carries the title, the name in large handwriting, the labels and the Coins. "Click anywhere to continue" under it, and over that, in cream handwriting, "/reload to keep it safe" (ANN-R97).
- **Party toast:** (ANN-R107) a compact strip, 380 × 50, the same for every groupmate in every toast style: the item's icon in a 34 medallion at the left, the groupmate's name (ANN-R105, "Kukli Emnabi") in the print face's capitals as its kicker, the moment's line in the hand under it ("Caught a Legend · Keefer's Angelfish"), cut to its room, and the mug, 28 units, at the right edge, 10 in from it. A moment's line: Legend "Caught a Legend · {item}"; colour "Caught a rare colour · {variety}" (or "an epic colour"), or the item's name when Core can't name the variety; Monster "Monster-size record · {item} · {length}" in the player's length unit; Hunt gold "Took gold in {Hunt}", or "Took gold in a Hunt" when the player's Hunts can't name it; Zone Master "Became Master of {zone}", the zone in the player's language, or "Zone Master" when the client can't name it; Prestige "Prestige {rank}"; Ledger "Ledger 100%". An item the client hasn't cached is left off with its " · ".
- **"While you were fighting":** a toast on a taller page listing the held headlines, a row each with a small medallion, up to 3, and "+N more" under them for the rest.
- **Session summary and Write-up cards:** a page torn from the ledger centre-screen until the Angler moves it, by its leather head or with a Shift-drag (ANN-R102), the same in every toast style: a leather head with the gilt title and a close mark, Core's numbers in the hand, the sections in ANN-R53 and ANN-R61 on the page's ruled lines (a moment's text in the hand at the right of its title's line, or, when the two don't fit there side by side, on the lines under the title, wrapped), scrolling past 12 lines, and a gilt close button at the page's foot. Closed with either button or Esc.

Look reference: the announcements art kit (`pagle-announce-kit`, its previews and its probe addon). The text above wins where they differ.

### Motion

The frames move as the art kit's motion has them (`pagle-announce-kit` v2, its `MOTION.md`). Each plays once, when the Announcement appears, and holds where it settles; drawn again for a toast style change, it carries on. A sample on the Curio Shop's stage stands still, and a card has none. With the **Animations** setting off, each appears as it settles. Times are from when it appears:

| Frame | Motion | Settled |
|---|---|---|
| Toast | It rises 26 units and fades in (0.56–0.98 s, overshooting a little); its name is written out left to right in 16 steps (0.98–1.82 s); its stamps slam down from 2.6x (1.89–2.31 s, overshooting); its coin turns in edge-on (2.31–2.66 s). A name line too long for its room then scrolls to and fro while it shows (ANN-R101). Its record ruler keeps its own count (ANN-R22), and its "New record" stamp slams down as it lands. A Rare or Epic variety's toast, the catch toast's too (VAR-R28), also gets one gleam across its page, left to right in the rarity's colour, added onto the paper, brightest midway and gone at the far end (0.7–1.3 s). | 2.66 s |
| Banner | The seal or Prestige frame drops from 2.2x (0–0.63 s, overshooting); the gilt tooling draws out from the seal (0.35–1.12 s); the rays fade in (0.35–1.4 s) and turn clockwise once in 60 s while it shows (ANN-R100). | 1.4 s (the rays never settle) |
| Splash | The rays turn clockwise once a minute (RAYS_TURN, ANN-R100) for as long as it shows. | never |
| Nod | It fades in. A Rare or Epic catch toast stepped down to a Nod (VAR-R26) also gets one gleam across its strip, left to right in the rarity's colour, added onto the paper, brightest midway and gone at the far end (0.1–0.7 s); it holds nothing up and plays nothing. | 0.3 s |

Each leaves as its time is up (ANN-R20): a toast or Nod fades out drifting up 12 units (a toast 0.4 s, a Nod 0.3 s), while the column closes up under it (0.3 s); a banner fades and shrinks to 96% about its top (0.5 s); a splash goes at once. With Animations off, each goes at once.

One `OnUpdate` drives every animation, Core's and each Module's (`adapter/Motion.lua`, with the keyframe maths and the player in `logic/Motion.lua`; Contracts), since `AnimationGroup` can't overshoot, step, ease each segment, or animate a width, a texture coordinate or a clip.

## Edge cases

- **ANN-R90 Module disabled, then enabled again.** A disabled Module raises nothing; its moments already stored stay and sort by their stored order (ANN-R53), and its lines stay stored but are left out of new cards (ANN-R51). Re-enabled, its catch-up raises no moments, and a Write-up section covers what that history earned (ANN-R60).
- **ANN-R91 Projection rebuild.** Raises no pop-ups; newly earned moments go to the summary only (ANN-R63).
- **ANN-R92 First build over existing history.** One Write-up card, no moments (ANN-R60–R62). A lost Module DB counts as a first build (LOG-R77).
- **ANN-R93 In combat.** ANN-R35–R37.
- **ANN-R94 Crash mid-Session.** `global.summary` and `global.cards` are saved with everything else, so moments raised before the last save survive and show as "Your last session" at the next login; later ones are lost with their Casts.
- **ANN-R95 Daily or weekly reset.** No rule here depends on it. A Tackle Board refill raises nothing; its login Nods land under ANN-R52.
- **ANN-R96 Uncharted or unconfirmed Catch.** An Uncharted Catch is the Ledger's `toast.low`, which replaces the catch toast (ANN-R13); its later reclassification credits arrive through ANN-R63. An unconfirmed catalogue fact changes nothing here: Modules decide whether it earns a moment.
