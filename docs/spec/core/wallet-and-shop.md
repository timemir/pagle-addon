# Core: Pagle Coins and the Curio Shop

> Status: Done · Milestones: M2 (wallet: earned totals, balance, Coins stamps, the balance in the compartment menu), M4 (Curio Shop, Look and Feel page, the first goods) · Sources: [Design the Pagle currency and shop](https://github.com/timemir/pagle-addon/issues/28#issuecomment-6125331898), [Prototype the Curio Shop](https://github.com/timemir/pagle-addon/issues/30#issuecomment-6125718197), [Prototype the Tackle Board](https://github.com/timemir/pagle-addon/issues/29#issuecomment-6126015781), [Prototype the Fish Journal](https://github.com/timemir/pagle-addon/issues/12#issuecomment-6112909660), [Decide how the spec is assembled](https://github.com/timemir/pagle-addon/issues/31#issuecomment-6128960652), [Decide the lore pipeline](https://github.com/timemir/pagle-addon/issues/34#issuecomment-6129762441); ADR 0001, 0003, 0007, 0009, 0015. Prototypes: `prototypes/curio-shop/` (variant B), `prototypes/curio-crate/` (the crate's opening)

Part of [Core](README.md). Core holds the Angler's **Pagle Coins** and runs the **Curio Shop** (ADR 0009).

## Purpose and boundary

**Owns:** the wallet (earned totals by source, spent total, balance), the goods registry, ownership and equipped looks, purchase entries, the Curio crate, the Curio Shop window, the Look and Feel settings page, and Core's own goods (toast styles, sound sets, bookplate crests).

**Doesn't own:** what a Module pays Coins for or how much (each Module's spec), the looks a Module's own goods apply to (each Module draws its own views), or when a Module's earned good is earned (the Ledger decides when its level bindings are earned).

## Rules

### Earning

- **SHOP-R1** Coins reward progress and achievements. There is no payment per Catch. A level-up and a Prestige count as progress: each pays once, when it is reached. Nothing pays per Catch, per hour or per Session, and no trickle tops up a slow day. Core itself pays nothing: no Coins for Casts or Catches. Stats and QoL pay nothing.
- **SHOP-R2** A paying Module keeps its own **lifetime Coins earned** in its projection (ADR 0004) and reports it with `M:ReportCoinsEarned(total)`: from its projection's `done` after every catch-up or rebuild (even when unchanged, so a lost `PagleDB` refills), and after each live entry that changes it. It never reports mid-rebuild.
- **SHOP-R3** Core stores the last total each Module reported in `PagleDB.global.coins.earned[<def.name>]`. A Module that is disabled or uninstalled keeps its last total, so nothing it paid is lost. If `PagleDB` itself is lost, a disabled Module's total is gone until it is enabled and reports again. The totals belong to the log's `logId` (`PagleDB.global.coins.logId`): when `PagleLog.logId` differs from it (a new log, LOG-R18), Core empties them at `ADDON_LOADED` and takes the new `logId`, so a new log never inherits the old one's totals; each Module's report after its rebuild fills them in again (as AXP-R3 does for Activity XP, [#278](https://github.com/timemir/pagle-addon/issues/278)).
- **SHOP-R4** Disabling a Module never lowers what it paid. Only a rebalance or data fix can recount a Module's total lower; then `earned` follows the new report, but the balance never goes below 0 (SHOP-R12) and nothing bought is taken back.
- **SHOP-R5** Coins show as a "+N" stamp on the Announcement the moment already earns (`coins` on the moment, ANN-R23) and as one Session summary line ("Pagle Coins +45"). Earning Coins never makes an Announcement of its own. Coins earned in history a Module catches up on or first builds from appear only as a line in its Write-up section, written by the Module (ANN-R60).

### Spending

- **SHOP-R10** A purchase appends a purchase entry (`U1`, LOG-R39) with the good's ID and the price paid. A later price change never moves a balance, and purchases are never revoked.
- **SHOP-R11** Core keeps `spent` and the set of purchased good IDs as a Core projection over the log (LOG-R78). Only the first `U1` for a good counts; any later one for the same good is ignored. A Curio crate's `U1` (good `core.crate`) is the exception: every one counts, and adds its price less its payback to `spent` (SHOP-R50, SHOP-R51).
- **SHOP-R12** **Balance** = Σ earned − spent, shown as **0** when that is negative. While it is negative ("held"), the shop shows one quiet line: "Your balance is 0 while Pagle re-counts earlier Coins. New Coins fill that in first." It never mentions debt or a negative number.
- **SHOP-R13** **Buy** is enabled only when the good is for sale (or an earned good past its fallback date, SHOP-R28), not owned, its Module is enabled, the balance covers the price, and no projection (Core's or any Module's) is updating. Each purchase is one good and one confirmation. The Curio crate's Buy has its own first condition (SHOP-R54).

### Goods

- **SHOP-R20** A **good** is a look for one of Pagle's own views, in one **slot**. Goods never carry meaning: titles, Fly Box flies, stars, Legends, Prestige and set emblems are never goods. Words drawn into a good's art ("WANTED" on Bounty Poster, "No.7" on Canvas Field Pad, "FLIES · HOOKS" on Tackle Tin, "the Long Haul" on Scrimshaw Panel, "NO.0042 · PAID" on Goblin Ticker, the I–IV on Five-Set Seal) are decoration, part of the texture, not strings: they are outside CORE-R8, never in `ns.L`, and stay English in every locale.
- **SHOP-R21** Modules register their own slots and goods with `M:RegisterSlot` and `M:RegisterGood`, at any time after `RegisterModule`; Core registers its own. Slot and good IDs are `<def.name lowercased>.<name>` (`core.` for Core's), match `[%w_.]+`, and never change once shipped.
- **SHOP-R22** Every good is exactly one of:
  - **default**: free and owned by everyone; each slot has exactly one
  - **for sale**: price tier 1 to 6 (250, 400, 900, 3,000, 10,000 or 25,000 Coins). Tier 6 has no goods yet; an empty tier is fine (Tunables), and the Curio crate still rolls it, as a crate tier with nothing left (SHOP-R51)
  - **earned**: not for sale (until a fallback date, if it has one: SHOP-R28); owned when its Module says so (`isEarned()`), with an `earnedText` saying how: a string ("Angler Level 30"), or a function the shop calls each time it draws the good while it isn't owned, returning the row's tag and, optionally, the stage's note ("Expert · 88%", "Earned when … Next: …"). An earned good may also have `progress()`, which the shop calls each time it draws the good on the stage while it isn't owned: a list of dots `{ color = { r, g, b }, filled = boolean }`, drawn on the stage (UI). Neither function's result is ever cached, and Core never calls either to decide ownership (SHOP-R30); one that raises is reported and its texts or dots are left out, and so is a dot without a colour of three numbers.
  - **crate only**: never for sale and never earned; owned when a Curio crate gives it (SHOP-R50). Only Core registers these, at most two in all (Tunables).
- **SHOP-R23** Registration raises a Lua error and ignores the call for: a duplicate ID, an ID without the Module's prefix, an unknown slot, a slot registered by a different Module (or by Core, for a Module, except an earned good in a Core slot registered with `openToEarned = true`: `core.toaststyle`, `core.soundset`, `core.crest` and `core.binding`, SHOP-R25), a second default in a slot, a good with more than one of `default` / `tier` / `isEarned` / `crate`, or none of them, a `crate` good from a Module, a `fallbackTier` or `fallbackFrom` on a good that isn't earned, or one without the other (SHOP-R28), a `progress` on a good that isn't earned, a slot's `groups` that aren't a list of `{ key, name }` with distinct keys matching `[%w_]+` and non-empty names, and a good's `group` that isn't one of its slot's keys (SHOP-R27). At `ModulesChanged`, a slot with no default is reported through `geterrorhandler()`, and its first registered good acts as the default. Only `default = true` marks a default; `default` is `true` or `false`, and anything else is a mistake.
- **SHOP-R24** Stock never rotates and nothing is limited-time, and the shop sells: it lists what can be bought, and nothing that can't. A slot's list holds its default, its goods for sale, its crate-only goods, and each earned good from its fallback date on (SHOP-R28), when it can be bought; never an earned good before then, owned or not. Every good, earned ones included, is in the Fish Journal's Collection ([ledger.md](../ledger.md) LED-R166, [#400](https://github.com/timemir/pagle-addon/issues/400)), which is where earned goods are seen, followed and put on, and Look and Feel equips any owned good (SET-R51).

  **Tier principles.** A tier says what a good for sale is, whatever its type, so new content is placed without argument (`tier` is 1 to 5 in the registry, SHOP-R22; tier 6 has no price yet):

  | Tier | Stands for | Motion |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | Entry look: a clean, simple design, one accent colour | none |
  | 2 | Richer static art: detailed illustration, texture, a themed frame | none |
  | 3 | The first animated tier: one looping ambient effect (glow, shimmer, slow rotation) | one layer |
  | 4 | A showpiece: several layers, or an effect with a distinctive entrance | multi-layer |
  | 5 | Epic: a signature animation, a full scene, unmistakable | rich, multi-layer |
  | 6 | Prestige: one-of-a-kind, what a veteran shows off | the best of the shop |

  - A good that moves is never below tier 3. A good that breaks a principle is moved to the tier that fits; its motion is never removed. Where a good is clearly a showpiece, its tier rises, which is why Rune-Plate, a bronze plate that stands still, is tier 3.
  - **Toast styles**: richer is the art, then the animation. Tier 1 is a clean page in one accent; tier 2 a detailed illustration, texture or themed frame; tier 3 a composed style with one looping ambient effect; tier 4 several moving layers or a distinctive entrance. A toast style is animated when it is composed (ANN-R82) and a layer of any of its rungs carries motion; the entrance of the page, ink and stamp is the same for every style (ANN-R80) and doesn't count. `spec/Pagle/logic/ToastTiers_spec.lua` pins Core's toast styles' tiers and fails if an animated one for sale is below tier 3, so a later move is deliberate. Tier 5 holds none yet: epic toast styles are still to come.
  - **Sound sets**: richer is how complex or layered the sound is, from a single clean sound at tier 1, up through a short phrase or a texture, to a layered scene at tier 4 and above.
  - **Bindings**: richer is the material, from a plain cloth or board at tier 1 to a worked or precious one (shagreen, bone, pearl, a trophy case) at the top. A binding doesn't move, so what raises its tier is the craft and the richness of its material.
  - **Covers, frames and the like** (a Module's): richer is how worked the art is, with motion from tier 3, as for a toast style.

- **SHOP-R26** Core caches every registered good in `PagleDB.global.goods`: name, slot, slot label and category, Module, icon, `default`, tier, `crate`, fallback tier and date, description and, for earned goods, the `isEarned()` result as of the last registration or `RefreshGoods` (SHOP-R31). The cache is never pruned for a good that is owned.
- **SHOP-R27** A slot may declare ordered **groups** at registration, `groups = { { key, name }, … }`, `name` interface text, and a good of it may name one in `group`. The shop lists a grouped slot's goods with no group first, then each group's under a header with its name, in the slot's order (UI); a header with none of its goods left by the search, filter or chip isn't shown. A slot's groups are fixed when it registers, so a good can only name a group already there (SHOP-R23). Groups order the list only: owned counts, filters and ownership ignore them. A group holding only goods the shop doesn't list (SHOP-R24) never heads a row.
  **Order within a slot, or within each of its groups** (the list only): the slot's default first (the registered default, or the first registered good standing in for it, SHOP-R23); then the goods for sale (SHOP-R22), cheapest tier first, tier 1 at the top and tier 6 at the bottom, goods of one tier in registration order (the sort is stable); then every listed good with no price, the earned goods on sale at their fallback price and the crate-only ones, in registration order. A good is for sale only if it has a `tier`: an earned good with a fallback price (SHOP-R28) is still earned, and sorts among the earned. A Module's earned goods in a Core slot open to them therefore list after Core's goods for sale, as they register. The owned counts, filters, group headers and the Curio crate's row do not change with the order. `Pagle.GetSlotGoods` and what reads it, the Collection, keep registration order (SHOP-R21).
- **SHOP-R28** **Fallback price.** An earned good may carry `fallbackTier` and `fallbackFrom` (a server time, Unix seconds). From `fallbackFrom` on, while it isn't owned, it can also be bought at that tier's price: the shop shows its earned tag and a price tag both, and the stage's note ends "Or buy it for N". Buying appends `U1` like any purchase (SHOP-R10), so the good is owned whatever its Module says; earning it later changes nothing. Only the second and third rounds' earned goods have one (the Ledger's toast styles and bindings of LED-R118, its race and faction styles of LED-R164, and Sonar Scope, SHOP-R29), which ship in one release: fallback tier 4, from 90 days after the release that ships them. Each owner's `logic/` holds that release's server time as a constant, `nil` until the release sets it; while it is `nil`, its goods register with neither field, and so have no fallback yet. An earned good from before the second round never gets one. A fallback never goes off sale again, so nothing is limited-time (SHOP-R24). An earned good with a fallback is still earned, so it never joins the Curio crate's pool (SHOP-R50).
- **SHOP-R29** **Sonar Scope** (`core.toaststyle.sonar`) is Core's one earned good, `earnedText` "Link the companion": its `isEarned()` is true once the wallet projection has applied a Companion entry (`K1`, LOG-R37), the mark that the companion's data addon passed its signature check ([pushed-content.md](pushed-content.md), PUSH-R13). Core refreshes it right after appending `K1` and from the wallet projection's `done`. Unlinking the companion later doesn't take it back.

### The Curio crate

- **SHOP-R50** The **Curio crate** is sold on its own row at the very top of the shop's list, above every category's slots, under the label "Curio crate", at 1,500 Coins (a Tunable of its own, not a price tier's). It belongs to no slot or category: it isn't a good (SHOP-R20), it is never owned, equipped or put away, and it stays at the top whichever category chip is on. Buying one appends `U1|<header>|core.crate|<price>` and opens it at once: it gives one good of the **crate pool** that the Angler doesn't own yet, or, when it rolls a crate tier with nothing left to give, pays Coins back (SHOP-R51). It never gives a good already owned. Everything below that speaks of the crate's price means the price in that entry. A good given by a crate is owned as a purchase is (SHOP-R30), never revoked, and shows in the shop as "From a crate": the row's tag of a crate-only good not owned yet, and a note on the stage of a good a crate gave. The row shows under every chip and filter; only a search its label doesn't match leaves it out, and it leaves the shop once every pool good is owned (SHOP-R54).
  The **crate pool** is the whole shop: every good for sale (SHOP-R22), Core's and every Module's, and every crate-only good; never a default or an earned good, not even one past its fallback date (SHOP-R28). It is a list in Core's `logic/` (Tunables), each row a good's ID, its **crate tier** and its **kind**, in a frozen order (SHOP-R53). A good's crate tier is its price tier, 1 to 6, as it joins the pool, or **Curio** for a crate-only good. Its kind names the emblem the reel shows for it (UI › Motion and sound): toast style, sound set, binding, tracker frame or crest, after its slot (every tracker frame slot is "tracker frame"); a kind with no good in the pool needs no emblem. A repo check fails when a good for sale or crate only is missing from the pool, so a new good needs one row there and nothing else.
  A pool good need not be registered: the draw reads the log and the pool alone (SHOP-R51), so a crate can give a good whose Module is off, or whose slot hasn't registered yet, and it is owned from that crate on (`Pagle.IsOwned` is true by the log); the shop lists it, and Use can equip it, once it registers. The opening names it and shows its icon from Core's goods cache (SHOP-R26); a good with no cache entry, its Module never yet loaded, turns over to its kind's emblem, "A new look", and the line "It shows here once its Module is on.", and the crate stays selected.
- **SHOP-R51** **The draw is the log's.** The wallet projection works out each crate from the log, so every rebuild gives the same result (ADR 0004). The **candidates** of a crate tier are the pool goods of that crate tier that are in the pool by the crate's time (SHOP-R53) and not owned by an earlier `U1` or crate, in pool order. The odds are fixed and never rescaled (Tunables, per mille): a crate tier with nothing left keeps its odds. With `h` LOG-R66's frozen hash and `<entry ID>` the crate `U1`'s (LOG-R13), worked in whole numbers so every step is exact below 2^53: the **tier** is the first in the order 1 to 6, then Curio, whose running odds satisfy `running × 2^32 > h(<entry ID>) × 1000`, or Curio when the pity applies (SHOP-R52). If that tier has `n` candidates, the crate gives the one at position `⌊h(<entry ID> .. "/good") × n / 2^32⌋ + 1` in pool order; if it has none, the crate gives no good and pays back (below). Whether a good's Module is enabled doesn't change the candidates; a good of a disabled Module goes to "Put away" (SHOP-R35).
  **Payback.** A crate that rolls a crate tier with no candidate (every good of it owned, or none in the pool, as tier 6 today) pays back `min(the tier's price, the crate's price)` Coins; for Curio, the crate's price. With today's numbers: 250 / 400 / 900 / 1,500 / 1,500 / 1,500, and Curio 1,500. A payback lowers what the crate cost in the wallet projection: the crate's spend is its price less its payback (SHOP-R11). It is never Coins earned: Core still pays nothing (SHOP-R1) and no Module's total changes. The log holds only the `U1` (SHOP-R10); the projection works the payback out.
- **SHOP-R52** **Curio pity.** While a crate-only good is a candidate, when the crates since the last one that gave a crate-only good (or since the Angler's first crate) number 99 and none gave one, the 100th crate's tier is Curio whatever the hash, and it then gives a crate-only candidate by the good hash, as SHOP-R51 has it. A crate that gives any other good, or pays back, adds one to the count; one that gives a crate-only good sets it to 0. The projection keeps the count (`dry`, Data). A pity crate's reel card is the Curio "?" (UI › Motion and sound).
- **SHOP-R53** **Frozen once shipped.** From 1.0, the hash, the odds, the pool's order, each pool row's crate tier, the payback rule and the pity count never change for crates already in the log (as LOG-R68); the payback rule reads the price tiers as they stand at 1.0, so a later price change never moves a balance (SHOP-R10). The pool's first rows are the goods for sale and crate only at 1.0, in the shop's order (Core's slots as SHOP-R25 lists them, then each Module's in Module order, each slot's goods in registration order); a good joins only at the pool's end, with the server time of the release that adds it (`since`), and is a candidate only for crates bought from then on. A later change to a good's price tier leaves its crate tier as it joined. At most two goods are ever crate only. Before 1.0 the draw may still change, as nothing is published (README › Rule IDs): a change bumps the wallet rules version (2 from the whole-shop crate), which rebuilds the projection.
- **SHOP-R54** **Buying a crate.** Buy is enabled while any pool good is still a candidate and SHOP-R13's other conditions hold. The stage shows the crate and, in place of a description, its odds: a line for each crate tier, its colour, its name, its goods left ("12 left") or, with none left, "pays back N", and its chance as a percent at the right ("40%", "2.5%"); then, while a crate-only good is a candidate, "A Curio within N more crates" (N is 100 less the count of SHOP-R52, at least 1; "A Curio in the next crate" at 1). The bill of sale reads "Open a Curio crate?" with the price and the line "One look you don't own yet, by chance; the chances are on the crate.", and has no "Use it now" box: the opening offers it (SHOP-R55). Once the Angler owns every pool good, the crate's row leaves the list, under every chip, filter and search, and the stage shows the list's first good if the crate was selected; a release whose new good joins the pool brings the row back. A crate is opened from the bill only: one crate, one confirmation, never several at once.
- **SHOP-R55** **Opening.** Bought at the click, the crate opens over the case (UI › Motion and sound): its reel lands on the result's card. For a good, the card turns over to show it, its name and its crate tier, then "Use it now", which equips it, and "Back to the shop"; either closes the opening onto the shop with the good selected on the stage. For a payback, the coin card turns over to "+N Coins" and the crate tier's name, the purse counts up by N, with none of the tier's fanfare (UI › Motion and sound), and "Back to the shop" is the only button, leaving the crate selected. With the Animations setting off, the result shows at once, with the same buttons. No Announcement follows (as SHOP-R10's purchases).

### Owning and equipping

- **SHOP-R30** `Pagle.IsOwned(goodID)` is true for defaults of registered slots, purchased goods, and earned goods whose cached `isEarned()` result is true. Core never calls `isEarned()` on an ownership check; it reads the cache, whether or not the Module is enabled.
- **SHOP-R31** Core calls a Module's `isEarned()` functions only when the good is registered and when the Module calls `M:RefreshGoods()`. A Module calls it after a live entry that could change an earned good, and from its projection's `done`; never mid-catch-up or mid-rebuild, so looks don't flicker.
- **SHOP-R32** Each slot has one **equipped** good, stored as a Core setting (`PagleDB.profile.appearance[slot]`). `Pagle.SetEquipped(slot, goodID)` accepts only an owned good of that slot. `Pagle.GetEquipped(slot)` returns the stored good if it is owned and registered, and the slot's default otherwise. That fallback happens on reading only: the stored choice is never cleared, so it comes back when its Module does.
- **SHOP-R33** `AppearanceChanged(slot, goodID)` fires whenever the result of `GetEquipped(slot)` changes, whatever the reason (a `SetEquipped`, a Module enabled or disabled, an earned good lost or regained). Each Module redraws its own views from `Pagle.GetEquipped`; Core redraws its frames and picks sounds from its own slots.
- **SHOP-R34** While the wallet projection is updating, `IsOwned` answers from the owned set saved before the update started, so looks don't flicker to defaults.
- **SHOP-R35** A disabled Module's goods for sale leave the shop. Its purchased goods stay owned and are listed under "Put away: Module off", from the cache. Its earned goods stay owned too; the shop never lists them (SHOP-R24), and the Collection shows them put away (LED-R172). Its defaults are neither listed nor counted. Its slots don't appear in Settings.
- **SHOP-R36** **What a view of every good reads** (the Ledger's Collection, LED-R166). Core offers, each answering from the registry and the cache at the call, never cached by Core between calls and never changing anything:
  - `Pagle.GetSlots()`: every registered slot of Core and of an On Module, in the shop's order (Core's first, then by `def.order`), as `{ id, label, category, module, groups }`;
  - `Pagle.GetSlotGoods(slot)`: every registered good of the slot, earned ones included, in registration order (the shop filters it itself, SHOP-R24);
  - `Pagle.GetGood(goodID)`: its cached fields (Contracts), its slot's `category` among them (as `GetSlots` gives it, so it holds for a good of a disabled Module too), with what it is and what it costs: `kind` (`"default"`, `"sale"`, `"earned"` or `"crate"`), `tier`, `price` (what it costs now if the shop sells it now: its tier's price, or from `fallbackFrom` its fallback price; `nil` otherwise), `fallbackPrice` and `fallbackFrom`, `group`, `purchased` (true only for a `U1` of the good itself), `fromCrate` (true when it is owned because a Curio crate gave it, SHOP-R50) and `ownedAt` (the server time of that `U1`, or of the crate that gave it, for a good owned either way; a crate that paid Coins back (SHOP-R51) gave no good and sets none);
  - `Pagle.GetEarnedProgress(goodID)`: for an earned good of an On Module not yet owned, `{ tag, note, have, need }`: its `earnedText` (calling the function each time, as the shop does, SHOP-R22) and its `earnedCount()` when it has one; `nil` otherwise, or when the call raises (reported, as SHOP-R22);
  - `Pagle.GetPutAwayGoods()`: every cached good of disabled Modules that isn't a default, owned or not, from the cache, so the Collection can draw each one put away and count it; a view reads each one's ownership with `Pagle.IsOwned`. The shop's "Put away" is this list narrowed to the goods a purchase or a crate gave (SHOP-R35);
  - `Pagle.DrawGoodPreview(frame, goodID, size)`: draws the good's look into any frame as the stage does (its `preview`, or its slot's, SHOP-R25), at `size` `"stage"` (the shop's whole stage, 390 × 190 from the frame's top and centred across it: the 370 × 132 well and the paper under it, which a sound set's note, a crest's and a binding's sit on, so the frame is at least that tall) or `"card"` (the 370 × 132 well scaled to fit the frame and centred in it), stilled as a preview is (announcements.md › Motion) but with its controls working (a sound set's ▶ Toast, ▶ Banner and ▶ Legend, a toast style's "Show it on screen"), so a view needs draw none of its own; a good with neither draws its icon in a medallion. It returns nothing and owns nothing it draws beyond the frame.

### Entry points

- **SHOP-R40** The Curio Shop opens from the compartment menu entry "Curio Shop" (CORE-R50), from `/pagle shop` (a toggle), from `Pagle.OpenShop(goodID?)`, and from the Look and Feel page's "Open the Curio Shop" button. `OpenShop` selects that good; an unknown ID selects nothing; an earned good the shop doesn't list (SHOP-R24) opens the Collection on it instead, through the first On Module's `def.collection` (CORE-R45), and with none opens the shop selecting nothing. That hand-over opens no shop, so it works while restricted too (SHOP-R41); a good of a disabled Module selects it in "Put away" if owned, otherwise nothing. Esc closes it.
- **SHOP-R41** While restricted, every entry point that would open the shop prints "Pagle: the Curio Shop opens after combat." instead; `Pagle.OpenShop` on an earned good the shop doesn't list opens the Collection, not the shop, and opens it all the same (SHOP-R40; with no Module offering one, the shop would open, so the line prints). At `RestrictionChanged(true)`, an open shop and its bill of sale (the Buy confirmation) close; nothing is queued.

The shop's sounds are the client's own sound kits (`PlaySound(id, "SFX")`), UI foley that is the same in every sound set (sound sets are the Announcements', ANN-R70); they follow the player's SFX volume and mute, and Pagle has no setting of its own for them (ANN-R73). The case opening and closing play `AUCTION_WINDOW_OPEN` (5274) and `AUCTION_WINDOW_CLOSE` (5275); Buy on the bill, once it buys, `IG_BACKPACK_COIN_OK` (865); a click on the dimmed Buy `IG_MAINMENU_OPTION_CHECKBOX_OFF` (857). The seal landing (`PUT_DOWN_GEMS`, 1204, at 0.4 s) and the purse counting down (`LOOT_WINDOW_COIN_SOUND`, 120, at 0.8 s) are beats of the motion, and play only with Animations on; as a crate opens, the purse's at 0.25 s.

The crate's art and sounds are not the client's. The **crate kit** (the crate in parts, the reel's frame, line, bobber and hook, the kinds' emblems, the Curio card and the coin card, the rays, burst, beam, spark, dust and straw) is rendered from the prototype's SVG and CSS by a repo script into one Core atlas under `media/shop/`, not drawn by hand. ### The pacing table

The shop is paced for a **casual** Angler: 1.5 hours a day, 180 Catches an hour, earning about 170 Coins per fishing hour (the Refresh tickets of [#405](https://github.com/timemir/pagle-addon/issues/405) assumed). Hours below are hours fished.

| Milestone | Target hours |
|---|---|
| First purchase | under 1 |
| 25% of the items | about 40 |
| 50% | about 100 |
| 75% | about 200 |
| Tiers 1–4 | about 430 |
| Tiers 1–5 | about 720 |
| First tier-6 good | about 870 |

**Price rule:** a tier's price is the casual Angler's average Coins per fishing hour (about 170) times the target hours per item of that tier. A dedicated or min-max Angler may earn fewer Coins per fishing hour, but reaches each milestone sooner by the calendar. The payouts of the payout table above (and in each Module's spec) are the constants that make the table hold: `spec/coin_economy_spec.lua` pins them, with the prices, so a content or price change can't move the pacing silently. Tier 6 has no goods yet; its milestone is the target for the first one, once added. An empty tier 5 or 6 is valid: nothing iterates tiers and requires goods in them.

## Edge cases

- **SHOP-R90 Module disabled, then enabled again.** While disabled: its last earned total still counts (SHOP-R3), its goods for sale leave the shop, owned ones move to "Put away" (SHOP-R35), and `GetEquipped` falls back to defaults for its slots without clearing the stored choice (SHOP-R32). Re-enabled: it reports its total after catch-up (with a Write-up line for Coins earned meanwhile), its goods return, and its stored looks come back.
- **SHOP-R91 Projection rebuild.** A Module's rebuild may change its earned total; the balance follows, held at 0 if needed (SHOP-R12). The wallet's own projection rebuilds from `U1` entries, without flicker (SHOP-R34). Buying waits until no projection is updating (SHOP-R13).
- **SHOP-R92 First build over existing history.** A late-enabled Module reports its whole earned total at once; it shows as a line in its Write-up section, not as stamps.
- **SHOP-R93 In combat.** SHOP-R41; reports and balance updates continue.
- **SHOP-R94 Crash mid-Session.** A purchase is a log entry like any other, so a purchase not yet saved is lost with the session; the good is simply not owned at the next login.
- **SHOP-R95 Daily or weekly reset.** No rule here depends on it.
- **SHOP-R96 Uncharted or unconfirmed Catch.** Not relevant: Coins come from Module achievements, which credit Uncharted Catches only once classified.
