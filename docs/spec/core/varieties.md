# Core: Fish varieties

> Status: In progress · Prefix `VAR` · Sources: the varieties kit (`pagle-varieties-kit.zip`: its SPEC, the catalogue page and the "Fish crests (earned)" and "Fish Journal · the varieties spread" boards of the Zone crests canvas, signed off by the dev on 22 Oct 2026), and the dev's grilling of it the same day; ADR 0001, 0010

Part of [Core](README.md). Every fish Pagle sizes comes in **ten varieties**: an everyday look, rarer colourings and one Epic specimen. Each Catch is one of them, chosen from its Catch ID. Collecting all ten of a fish earns that fish's **crest**; collecting all of them earns the Ledger's rarest title.

## Purpose and boundary

**Owns (Core):** which Catches have a variety, each Species' ten in their frozen order, the rarity odds, the variety of a Catch (decoded, never stored), Core's variety projection (which varieties each Species has been caught in), the 37 earned **fish crests** in Core's `core.crest` slot, the variety on the catch toast and the Nod it steps down to (amending ANN-R13), and the variety art and lore.

**Owns (the Ledger, amending ledger.md):** the Journal's varieties spread, the moments for a new variety and a completed fish, the Ledger completion part, and the `complete.varieties` title. The Ledger keeps its own record of varieties caught in its own projection (it never reads Core's), so each side rebuilds on its own (ADR 0001).

**Doesn't own:** Simulated Size (LOG-R65–R68, unchanged, and independent of the variety), XP and Coins (varieties pay neither, SHOP-R1), the crest slot's other goods (SHOP-R25).

## Rules

### Which Catches, and the ten

- **VAR-R1** A Catch has a **variety** when it has a Simulated Size (LOG-R65) and its item is not a Legend. Trophies, Sub-collection items, Noise, Uncharted items, Legends and item `0` have none. An Uncharted Catch gains its variety when a release classifies it as a Species (as LOG-R96 for size).
- **VAR-R2** Each Species with a variety has exactly **ten**, in a fixed order, listed in `data/forever/varieties.toml` (Data). Position sets the **rarity**: 1–4 **Common**, 5–7 **Uncommon**, 8–9 **Rare**, 10 **Epic**. A variety's ID is `<speciesID>.<key>` (`6359.phoenix-fin`). The generator fails when a sized, non-Legend Species has no entry, an entry is for anything else, has other than ten keys, or a key repeats within a Species (DATA-R132, its `varieties` switch on from the varieties release).
- **VAR-R3** The **variety percentile** `v` of a Catch is LOG-R66's hash (FNV-1a 32-bit, then `fmix32`) over the Catch ID string with `#variety` appended, `v = h / 2^32`. The salt makes it independent of the size percentile: a big fish is no likelier to be a rare one. The variety is the first position `i` whose cumulative weight exceeds `v × 10000`, with the weights (per 10,000 Catches of a Species) 1750, 1750, 1750, 1750, 734, 733, 733, 350, 350, 100: Common 70%, Uncommon 22%, Rare 7%, Epic 1%.

  Test vectors (`h` after `fmix32`, over the Catch ID followed by `#variety`):

  | Catch ID | `h` | `v` | Position | Rarity |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | `Player-4395-0A1B2C3D-1791731747-0:1` | 4121951395 | 0.959717 | 9 | Rare |
  | `Player-4395-0A1B2C3D-1791731747-0:2` | 141524587 | 0.032951 | 1 | Common |
  | `Player-4395-0A1B2C3D-1791731762-1:1` | 4262029481 | 0.992331 | 10 | Epic |
  | `Player-1-00000001-1700000000-0:1` | 218561979 | 0.050888 | 1 | Common |

  A test draws 200,000 synthetic Catch IDs (sequential times, 2 items per Cast) and checks each position's share is within 0.3 percentage points of its weight (the kit measured 17.44–17.59, 7.29–7.39, 3.50–3.51 and 1.03) and that `v` and the size percentile `u` of the same Catch are uncorrelated (the kit measured 0.0005).
- **VAR-R4** Core computes the variety whenever it decodes a record, from the Catch ID and the current catalogue, as it does the size; it is never stored. Records carry it as `variety = { id, index, rarity }` (`rarity` one of `"common"`, `"uncommon"`, `"rare"`, `"epic"`), beside `size`. The catalogue carries each Species item's ten keys in order as `varieties` (`Pagle.GetItem(itemID).varieties`).
- **VAR-R5** The hash, the salt, the weights and each Species' order are **frozen once shipped** (as LOG-R68, ADR 0010): a Catch's variety never changes. The variety hash is its own frozen copy of LOG-R66's steps 1 and 2 (`ns.VarietyHash`), never a call into Size's, so a change to the Simulated Size (its rules version, its Reach) can never move a variety. A freeze spec (`spec/Pagle/logic/Variety_spec.lua`) pins the table above and, for each cumulative cut point, the two synthetic Catch IDs closest below and at or above it. A variety's name, description and art can change in any release; its key and position never do. A removed Species keeps its ten.

### Core's variety projection and the fish crests

- **VAR-R10** Core keeps a **variety projection** over the log (a Core projection, LOG-R78, bound at login step 1 after the wallet's and ahead of every Module's): for each Species, the set of positions caught, from every caught Cast's items that have a variety, keyed by the Species in the variety's ID (a folded item's Catches count for its Species). It is saved in `PagleDB.global.projections.varieties` with its `rulesVersion` (1) and rebuilt when the catalogue fingerprint changes (DATA-R71). The backend folds it too (`ns.BindCoreProjections`, LOG-R79).
- **VAR-R11** `Pagle.GetVarieties(speciesID)` returns the Species' ten, in order: `{ id, key, index, rarity, name, description, art }`, with `name` and `description` from lore (`ns.L`) and `art` the variety's texture and texel rectangle (UI: `{ texture, left, right, top, bottom }`, texture coordinates ready for `SetTexCoord`). It is the Species' static list: it never says which were caught, and showing an uncaught one's name, description or colours is the caller's choice (VAR-R14, VAR-R24 never do). `Pagle.GetVarietiesCaught(speciesID)` returns Core's caught positions as a fresh set `{ [index] = true }` each call, empty for a Species with none caught or without varieties and before the projection's first build has finished; while it rebuilds, it answers from the last stored state (LOG-R72). `Pagle.VARIETY_RARITIES` lists `{ key, name, weight }` in order.
- **VAR-R12** Core registers one **fish crest** per Species with a variety, in its `core.crest` slot and its `fish` group (SHOP-R27): `core.crest.fish.<speciesID>`, **earned** (SHOP-R22), owned once all ten of that Species have been caught (Core's projection), and kept once owned (VAR-R90). The 37 at release are listed in Data; they register at login step 1, after the catalogue and before the variety projection starts, in ascending Species ID. Its icon is the crest's own texture (`media/crests/fish/<speciesID>`), drawn like the zone crests (SHOP-R25). Its name is lore's "{fish} Crest" with `{fish}` the Species' item name as the client resolves it at runtime (`C_Item.GetItemNameByID`; DATA-R3: no game name ships), less a leading "Raw " on an enUS client ("Raw Spotted Yellowtail" makes "Spotted Yellowtail Crest"; the prefix is a locale string, and another locale's names are used as the client gives them), "Item {itemID}" until the client has it cached: Core asks the client for each fish it hasn't (`C_Item.RequestLoadItemDataByID`) and, at its `GET_ITEM_INFO_RECEIVED`, renames the good and its cached name (SHOP-R26). Its description is lore's own and never names the fish. The Curio Shop doesn't list fish crests (SHOP-R25): they are earned, not sold, and equipped from Look and Feel's Crests dropdown (SET-R51); an equipped one stays on the Journal bookplate (LED-R112), and the Every colour banner names it (VAR-R25).
- **VAR-R13** Amends SHOP-R22 and SHOP-R31 for Core's own earned goods: Core calls their `isEarned()` after its variety projection's `done` and after a live Catch that adds a variety; never mid-catch-up or mid-rebuild. The projection runs Core's internal hooks (`ns.OnVarietiesCaught(fn)`, logic/Wiring.lua) at exactly those points, every `done` (a catch-up's or a rebuild's, with or without a change) and each live Catch adding a position not caught before, so the fish crests refresh Core's goods from a hook. Their `earnedText` is a function, read each time the shop would draw one: "{n} of 10 varieties" (the row's tag, SHOP-R22); as the shop lists no fish crest (VAR-R12), it isn't drawn at present. An earned good of a Module keeps a plain string.
- **VAR-R14** An unearned fish crest's `progress` (SHOP-R22) is ten dots in variety order, filled in the rarity's colour when caught and ringed in it when not, and its `earnedText`'s note "{n} of 10 {Species name} varieties. Still out there: {count and rarity of the uncaught, rarest last, as "2 Common, 1 Rare, 1 Epic"}." It never names an uncaught variety (VAR-R27). The Curio Shop, which lists no fish crest (VAR-R12, SHOP-R25), doesn't draw either at present.

### In the Ledger (amending ledger.md)

- **VAR-R20 (LED-R70)** Ledger completion adds **every Species with varieties** (done: all ten caught), from the Ledger's own projection, removed Species left out of both sides. Its world is the Species part's (LED-R10: listed in a charted zone), so 35 parts at release: Zulian Mudskunk and Whimsyfin have varieties and crests but are on no charted zone's list. The `complete.varieties` title counts the same 35 (VAR-R22).
- **VAR-R21 (LED-R71)** The Angler page adds the part "Varieties" with "{fish complete} of {N}".
- **VAR-R22 (LED-R75)** Title `complete.varieties`, earned when every variety of every Species with varieties has been caught (a total above 0). Lore: "Keeper of Every Colour". It is meant to be the rarest title the Ledger gives.
- **VAR-R23 (LED-R140)** A caught Species' entry (the fish spread's left page, and the zone spread's right page with the fish selected) adds, under "First landed …", the **varieties card**: a raised paper card the page wide, with the first caught variety's art, "Varieties", "{n} of 10 found", a pip per variety in its rarity's ink (filled once caught, faint until then; an uncaught variety gives only its rarity, VAR-R27) and "See all ten »". The whole card opens the **varieties spread** (UI, LED-R151) on the first caught variety. A Species without varieties shows no card, and neither does one never caught (LED-R124).
- **VAR-R24** The varieties spread: left page kicker the Species' name, title "Its colours", "{n} of 10 caught" in the hand, then the ten as rows in order, each with a dotted rule under it: the variety's art (76 × 38), its name, its rarity as a pill in the rarity's colour, and "{count} caught" in the hand at the right. An uncaught row shows the art as a dark silhouette, "Undiscovered" in ghost ink in place of its name, the pill outlined and no count. Clicking a row selects it (a pale band). Right page: the selected variety (the first caught, or position 1), kicker "A {rarity} colour" in the rarity's ink ("An" before Uncommon and Epic: "An uncommon colour"), then for a caught one its name, its art large (340 × 170), its description in italic and "First landed {date} in {zone}, by {character}. {count} caught."; for an uncaught one "Not yet discovered", the silhouette large, no description, and "Not landed yet. About one Catch in {round(10000 / weight)}." At the foot, over a rule, the fish crest small with its name and, in the hand, "All ten." when complete, else "{10 − n} to go."
- **VAR-R25** Moments (raised by the Ledger with the Cast, ANN-R10), for a variety caught for the first time:
  - Common or Uncommon: a stamp "New variety" on the Cast's toast; with no other moment, a `toast.low` headline "New variety" with its own toast (VAR-R28): the default `toast` page, the Catch's size as its kicker, the fish's name and the variety's on its line, the stamp "NEW VARIETY" top right.
  - Rare: a `toast` headline "Rare variety", the variety's art in the bezel and its own toast (VAR-R28) as above, with the stamp "RARE VARIETY".
  - Epic: a `banner` "Epic variety", the variety's name, the art on the seal's ribbon. While the Extravaganza Gala is the equipped toast style, Core raises it as a `toast.high` instead, drawn as a toast announcing a variety (VAR-R28) with the Gala's burst of confetti, the variety's art in the medallion and the stamp "EPIC VARIETY" top right, in the epic ink, as a Rare variety's toast has RARE VARIETY (announcements.md ANN-R85); every other style keeps the banner.
  - The tenth of a Species: a `banner` "Every colour of {Species}", sealed with its fish crest (`seal = "crest"` showing the fish crest), plus the crest's "Earned" line.
  - The last of all: the `complete.varieties` title moment, as the other `complete.*` titles.
  None carries Coins or XP. These moments are where a variety's name, description and art are first shown (VAR-R27). Their rows and orders are LED-R97's, the art rides as the moment's `art` and the variety's name and rarity as its `variety` (announcements.md › Contracts), and a catch-up writes them up as LED-R94's "{n} varieties · {m} fish in every colour" (VAR-R91). The Common, Uncommon and Rare moments carry `ownStamp`, the Catch's size text as `kicker` and the fish's name as `variety.fish` (announcements.md › Contracts). A variety's toast, headline or riding on another moment's, is drawn as VAR-R28 says.
- **VAR-R26 (amends ANN-R13; #437)** The catch toast (ANN-R13) names its Catch's variety when that Catch has one: its text line is the fish's name, then the variety's, "Firefin Snapper · ◆ Coral" (the gem as VAR-R28 draws it), under the kicker "DECENT · 34.2 CM". The variety name is in its rarity's ink with a small gem in the rarity's colour before it; the medallion keeps the item's icon (#469: the variety's art fills a medallion only on the variety's own first-catch toast, VAR-R25, VAR-R28), its ring tinted in the rarity's colour. A Rare or Epic one's page gets one gleam across it as it enters (the rarity's colour). It has no "New variety" stamp: it is not the first catch. When the budget steps the catch toast down to a Nod (ANN-R31), the Nod reads "{item name} · {variety name} · {band} · {length}", as "Firefin Snapper · Coral · Decent · 34.2 cm", with the variety's name in its rarity's ink after the same gem, the same item icon in the medallion and the same ring, and the same gleam across its strip; it stays silent and never interrupts (ANN-R30). The rarity colours replace the toast style's ink only on the gem and the variety name (ANN-R80); a toast announcing a variety follows the same look (VAR-R28). A first catch of a variety is a moment of VAR-R25, which replaces the catch toast as ANN-R13 says, so the catch toast names only varieties already caught: Core names one only when its own projection (VAR-R10) held it before this Cast, the positions the Cast itself caught first left out whether the projection folded it before the toast (it is live before `CastEnded`, LOG-R73) or folds it after (it was updating; it then reads its last stored state, LOG-R72). Any other Catch's toast reads as one without a variety. With the Ledger off (VAR-R91) that keeps the catch toast from being the first to show a variety (VAR-R27).
- **VAR-R27 Discovery.** Until a variety is first caught, nothing names it or shows its colours: the varieties spread (VAR-R24) and a fish crest's texts and dots (VAR-R14) show its rarity, its silhouette and its odds only. Its name, description and art are first shown in its own moment (VAR-R25).
- **VAR-R28 A toast announcing a variety (amends ANN-R11, ANN-R22; #424, #437).** A toast, in any toast style (ANN-R80, ANN-R82), whose Announcement holds a variety's first-catch moment (VAR-R25, the `variety` field), whether as its headline (a Rare variety's) or riding on another moment's (a size record's, a New Species'), or is Core's catch toast naming a variety caught before (VAR-R26), is drawn as the stepped-down catch Nod of VAR-R26 is, with the rarity's colour (Common white, Uncommon green `#1eff00`, Rare blue `#0070dd`, Epic purple `#a335ee`, each with its darker ink on parchment, UI), and for every such toast alike:
  - **Medallion:** on a toast announcing a first catch (a Common, Uncommon or Rare variety's moment, VAR-R25), the variety's art cropped to the fish's head replaces the item icon, wherever its moment rides, on an opaque round disc filling the medallion's face (deep water, tinted a little by the rarity's colour: the art is transparent round the fish, so the world never shows through). The catch toast naming a variety caught before (VAR-R26) and its Nod show the Catch's item icon, on no disc: its moment carries no art. On every such toast the ring is tinted in the rarity's colour (Common's stays the style's).
  - **Name:** when the headline's line is about that fish (its own `icon` is the variety's item and it has a text line) or is the variety's own toast (below), the line is the fish's name, then a small gem in the rarity's colour rimmed in its ink, then the variety's name in its ink; on a toast about something else (a zone rank), the line is left as it is. The fish's name has priority over the variety's: if "{fish} ◆ {variety}" is wider than the line, the line's font first steps down (the gem with it) to 70% of the line's font; if it still doesn't fit, the variety's name is cut short with "..." (to 48 units at least), and the fish's name only after that, as a last resort. On a ruled toast the line ends before the ruler's landed number.
  - **Stamp:** the "New variety" stamp (its border and its word) is in the rarity's ink; every other stamp stays the style's.
  - **Its own toast (#422):** a Common, Uncommon or Rare variety's moment that headlines its Cast's toast (ANN-R11) is drawn on the `toast` page (464 × 92, the 84 medallion; a composed style's `toast` rung) even when its rung is `toast.low` (as every `toast.low` toast is, ANN-R80), and holds 6 s, as every toast does (ANN-R20). Its **kicker** is the Catch's Simulated Size, "{band} · {length}" in the Angler's unit, as the catch toast's (ANN-R13; a variety always has a size, VAR-R1), not its title; its **line** is the fish's name, then the gem and the variety's name in the rarity's ink ("Firefin Snapper · ◆ Coral"); and its **own stamp**, "NEW VARIETY" (Common, Uncommon) or "RARE VARIETY" (Rare), hangs top right in the rarity's ink as a riding variety's does, first, the stamps of the moments riding on it beside it (ANN-R11). The moment's `title` ("New variety", "Rare variety") is unchanged where it names the moment: the Session summary, the cards, the Write-up and the stamp text. Rare's gleam, the art and the ring are as above. The Interruption budget is unchanged: it ranks and counts as a `toast.low` (Common, Uncommon), and a Common or Uncommon one the budget steps down is a Nod (ANN-R31) with the old words ("New variety · Coral") and no own stamp. Epic's is a banner, with none of this.
  - **Gleam:** a Rare or Epic variety's toast gets one gleam across its page as it enters, as the Nod's (VAR-R26), in the rarity's colour; it plays nothing, holds nothing up and is left out with Animations off. (The catch toast plays the toast sound as any toast does, ANN-R70; the gleam adds none.)
  - A size record's toast carries none of this on its own: a record's size band is its ruler's label (ANN-R22), never part of the line.

  The rarity colours replace the style's ink only on the gem, the variety name, the ring and the stamp. The catch toast is the exception to "only a variety just caught" (#437): its variety is one caught before, its text line is the fish's name (fitted as above), then the gem and the variety's name, and it carries no "New variety" stamp, with the tinted ring and the gleam as VAR-R28 has them but the item's icon in the medallion in place of the art on its disc (#469). The first catch of a variety is still VAR-R25's moment, which replaces the catch toast (ANN-R13). A toast with no variety moment is drawn as the style has it, and never names or colours a variety not caught (VAR-R27).

## Data

- `data/forever/varieties.toml`: one table per Species, `[varieties.<speciesID>]` with `keys = [ten keys]` in frozen order (DATA-R131). Emitted into the catalogue on every item of the Species (a folded item too, as its typical length) as `varieties = { keys }`. Changing a key or its position fails the generator against the shipped list (`data/forever/varieties.lock`, DATA-R133), which `--lock` writes beside Hunts' `released.lock` from the 1.0 tag and which stayed empty until then (DATA-R73).
- Lore, `data/lore/core.toml`: `[variety."<speciesID>.<key>"]` with `name` (28) and `description` (200), a new Core kind `core.variety` in `manifest.toml` listing all 370; `[good."core.crest.fish.<speciesID>"]` under `core.good`, each with its own `description` and the one name pattern every fish crest shares, `name = "{fish} Crest"` (VAR-R12), its manifest entry allowing the `{fish}` placeholder (DATA-R108, DATA-R134). Core reads it as any good's, `ns.L["good.core.crest.fish.<speciesID>.name"]`, and replaces `{fish}` with the item name. `data/lore/ledger.toml`: `[title."complete.varieties"]`. Lore limits add `description` 200 for `variety`.
- **The generator fails** when a variety's or a fish crest's text holds an area's name from `data/db2/areas.csv` (any area, zones and subzones, matched as whole words with its capitals) or a Species item's name (whole words, ignoring case, with and without a leading "Raw "), so no game name ships through them (DATA-R3, DATA-R135). The kit's names made from part of a game name (a lake's, a town's, a fish's own word, a game metal's) were renamed with their keys before release (#338); nothing had shipped.
- `addons/Pagle/data/VarietyArt.lua`: `ns.VARIETY_ATLASES` (the two atlas paths), `ns.VARIETY_ART_SIZE = { w = 192, h = 96, atlas = 2048 }` (texels) and `ns.VARIETY_ART[speciesID]`, the ten images in variety order as `{ atlas, x, y }` (the atlas's index, the top left corner in texels), from the kit's `atlas.json`; positions, not keys, so a renamed key keeps its art.
- Saved: `PagleDB.global.projections.varieties` (Core, VAR-R10), the projection machinery's stored values (`lastSeq`, `logId`, `fingerprint`, `rulesVersion`, LOG-R70) with `projection = { bySpecies = { [speciesID] = { [index] = true } } }`; `{}` in `ns.PAGLEDB_DEFAULTS`. The Ledger's projection gains `varieties = { [speciesID] = { [index] = { count, first = { entryId, time, date, zone, char } } } }` (`time`, the first Catch's server time, since `LEDGER_RULES_VERSION` 8, for the backend's `varieties` and fish crests, LED-R155).
- **Gaffer Jack (6717) and Electropeller (6718)** are `junk`, not `questFish`: they are a goblin engineer's lost gadgets fished up for his quests, not fish. They have no Simulated Size, no varieties, no crest and no fish page; each has a Junk Museum plaque. 37 Species have varieties at release.

## UI

Drawn from the canvas boards (the kit's `canvas/`): the catalogue page's variety art, "Fish crests · all 39" (37 after the two gadgets left), "Curio Shop · fish crests earned and in progress", "The Angler · Ahi Crest pinned on Prestige VII" and "Fish Journal · the varieties spread". The kit's probe (`PagleArtVarieties/Varieties.lua`) is the layout reference, in UI units.

- **Variety art** is prerendered: each variety a 192 × 96 texel image, transparent around the fish, packed into two 2048 × 2048 atlases (`media/varieties/Varieties1`, `Varieties2`, BLP2 DXT5 with mips); `art` (VAR-R11) names the atlas and rectangle. The varieties spread draws a card's art 76 × 38 and the selected one 340 × 170; a first-catch toast's bezel crops it to the fish's head (VAR-R26, VAR-R28); the catch toast and its stepped-down Nod show the item's icon instead (#469).
- **Silhouette**: an uncaught variety's art desaturated and tinted black at 28% (`SetDesaturated(true)`, `SetVertexColor(0, 0, 0, 0.28)`), a soft shadow of the fish.
- **Fish crests**: 256 × 256 textures as the zone crests (silver rim instead of gilt, the fish's water as the field, ten gilt stars at the foot), drawn and pinned exactly as SHOP-R25 and LED-R146 describe for crests.
- **Rarity pills** on the varieties spread (VAR-R24, LED-R151): square-cornered, 70 × 15, filled in the rarity's ink (its darker colour, for parchment) when caught and outlined in it when not.
- **Rarity colours**, the game's own quality colours: Common `#ffffff` (ink `#6a5a44` on parchment), Uncommon `#1eff00` (`#2f6a2a`), Rare `#0070dd` (`#23407a`), Epic `#a335ee` (`#7a2f8a`). On parchment Pagle uses the darker ink of each.

## Edge cases

- **VAR-R90 A release reclassifies an item.** A Catch that becomes a Species gains its variety; one that stops being one loses it. Both projections rebuild with the fingerprint (DATA-R71); a crest once earned stays owned: its `isEarned()` keeps the cached result (SHOP-R26) once true.
- **VAR-R91 Ledger disabled.** Core still tracks varieties and still awards crests; the Ledger catches up on its own record when enabled (LOG-R72) and raises a Write-up, not moments (ANN-R60).
- **VAR-R92 A stack of fish in one loot slot.** One Catch, one variety, whatever its quantity (as size, LOG-R65).

## Decisions

Settled in the dev's grilling of the kit (22 Oct 2026):

1. **Gaffer Jack and Electropeller** are junk, not fish (Data): their quests call them a dropped box of gaffer jacks and a lost bag of electropellers.
2. **Art** ships prerendered (UI), about 14 MB.
3. **The catch toast** names the variety and shows its rarity, and so does the Nod it steps down to (VAR-R26; #437).
4. **Discovery**: an uncaught variety is never named or shown in colour (VAR-R27).
5. **No game names** in variety or crest lore, checked by the generator (Data); a fish crest's name is filled in at runtime (VAR-R12).
6. **A toast announcing a variety** shows its art in the medallion, its name after a gem, its stamp and ring in the rarity's colour, and a Rare or Epic one gleams once, whichever moment is the toast's headline (VAR-R28; the dev, 26 Oct, #424), the catch toast's variety caught before without the stamp (#437) and with the item's icon in the medallion, not the art (the dev, #469).
