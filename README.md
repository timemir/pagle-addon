<p align="center">
  <b>Pagle</b> is a fishing addon for World of Warcraft: Forever, named after Nat Pagle and meant to be the only fishing addon you need.<br>
  Progression and collection come first, with optional quality-of-life touches beside them.<br>
  <i>It watches what you catch and rewards it. It never fishes for you.</i>

Every Cast you make is written into one log, shared by every character on your account, and everything Pagle shows is read from it. Open any of it from Pagle's entry in the addon compartment by the minimap, or with `/pagle`.

## Get it

Pagle is one download of six addons. Unzip it so every folder lands in `Interface/AddOns`, then `/reload`.

> [!NOTE]
> Pagle runs on **World of Warcraft: Forever** only, and 1.0 is in English only.

## The Fish Journal

<img src="docs/readme/ribbon-fish-journal.png" alt="The Fish Journal" width="100%">

The Ledger keeps your life as an angler in the Fish Journal, a leather-bound book of parchment pages. Every Catch earns XP toward your Angler Level, doubled by Fresh Water after a break from fishing, and at the top you can Prestige for an emblem and climb again. Each Species earns up to three stars, each zone a Zone Mastery rank from Visitor to Master, and the Junk Museum, Treasures and the Trophy Room fill in as you go.

<p align="center"><img src="docs/readme/30-journal-home.jpg" alt="The Fish Journal's home: the waters you've fished, your Angler Level and what the Ledger holds" width="100%"></p>

Fish you haven't caught stay nameless. A riddle sets the scene, and a plain hint is there when you'd rather just know. Legends, the named catches you won't land on an ordinary day, arrive with a splash and a title to sign the Journal with. Your longest of each Species is kept as well, from Tiddler to Monster.

## Hunts

<img src="docs/readme/ribbon-hunts.png" alt="Hunts" width="100%">

Hunts are the catches worth planning for: a fish that bites by night, in one season or during an event, or a catch that only counts under conditions of its own, such as a grouper by moonlight. The Hunts board, a leather notebook, lays them out on a timeline of what's biting now, what opens soon and what comes later. Each Hunt completes in bronze, some go on to silver and gold, and finishing a themed set earns its title. The board counts your Casts at each Hunt without pretending to know the odds, and the Hunts you pin follow you in a tracker under the minimap.

## Pagle Wagers

<img src="docs/readme/ribbon-pagle-wagers.png" alt="Pagle Wagers" width="100%">

Pagle Wagers are Hunts themed on one or two zones and put on the table for about three days at a time. Land every Hunt's first mark before the wager closes to settle it. Any number can be on at once, each one starts fresh and pays again, and every wager you settle climbs a ladder of titles.

## Stats

<img src="docs/readme/ribbon-stats.png" alt="Stats" width="100%">

Stats keeps your own numbers in the Stats book: every Session, your lifetime totals, and what bites in each water you've fished. Your odds for each fish come only from your own Casts, and always say how many Casts they rest on. A Dry spell counts the Casts since you last caught something and says plainly when the wait is unusual. Every Cast is its own roll; nothing is owed. Watch up to three Dry spells on the live tracker. Stats shows numbers when you ask, and announces nothing.

## The Tackle Board

<img src="docs/readme/ribbon-tackle-board.png" alt="The Tackle Board" width="100%">

The Tackle Board is opt-in: a cork board where three Notices go up each day and an Expedition each week, such as "4 different Species in Feralas". Fish where you like, and they fill in as you go. They never expire, so a missed day banks rather than vanishes, and each one can be taken down once for a fresh one of another kind. Each day with a goal done keeps your Campfire burning, Kindling carries it through a day you couldn't make, and your longest Campfire is kept for good. Every goal done ties a new fly in your Fly Box.

## Moments

<img src="docs/readme/ribbon-moments.png" alt="Moments" width="100%">

When you earn something, Pagle says so in the moment. A toast rises and writes the fish's name out in ink while its stamps come down, a banner drops its seal for a new Angler Level, and a Legend gets the whole screen. The Interruption budget keeps it from talking over your fishing, Cozy mode holds everything for the end of the Session, and the Animations setting stills the motion if you'd rather it kept still. A Session that earned something ends with a summary card.

## The Curio Shop

<img src="docs/readme/ribbon-curio-shop.png" alt="The Curio Shop" width="100%">

Pagle Coins are paid for things achieved, never for sheer volume, and buy looks in the Curio Shop: toast styles, sound sets, Journal bindings, notebook covers and tracker frames. The stock never rotates, and nothing in it buys a title or a star.

## The Fishing Key

<img src="docs/readme/ribbon-fishing-key.png" alt="The Fishing Key" width="100%">

QoL gives you a Fishing key. Bind it, and each press does the next sensible thing: put on your fishing set, apply a lure, cast, reel, or open a clam between Casts. A double right-click casts too. While you fish it can turn the music and ambience down so you hear the splash, and switch auto-loot on, and it puts your settings back when you stop. A small button shows what the next press will do, and a quiet line tells you when your lures run out. One press is one action, and QoL never acts on its own.

<p align="center"><img src="docs/readme/90-fishing-key.jpg" alt="One key for the whole cast, and your fishing clothes" width="100%"></p>

## How It Works

<img src="docs/readme/ribbon-how-it-works.png" alt="How It Works" width="100%">

<p align="center"><img src="docs/readme/20-how-it-works.jpg" alt="You fish, one Catch log records it, and every book reads from it. It never fishes for you." width="100%"></p>

Pagle is one download of six addons: Pagle itself and five Modules.

| Addon | What it is |
|---|---|
| `Pagle` | The core. It records every Catch into one log and owns the moments, the Curio Shop and the settings. |
| `Pagle_Ledger` | The Fish Journal. |
| `Pagle_Hunts` | Hunts and Pagle Wagers. |
| `Pagle_Stats` | Sessions, lifetime totals, odds and Dry spells. |
| `Pagle_TackleBoard` | Notices, Expeditions, the Campfire and the Fly Box. |
| `Pagle_QoL` | The Fishing key. |

Turn any Module off in the AddOns list and nothing of it loads. Pagle keeps recording your Catches either way, so a Module you turn back on catches up on everything it missed, in one card.

If Angleur or Fishing Buddy is loaded too, QoL asks which one should keep your fishing key, and turns the other off. The rest of Pagle carries on whichever you pick.

## The Pagle Companion

<img src="docs/readme/ribbon-companion.png" alt="The Pagle Companion" width="100%">

The Pagle Companion is an optional desktop app, outside the game. It uploads your Catch log to the Pagle website and brings the website back into the game: fresh Pagle Wagers from its schedule, in place of the addon's own rotation, and Published Anglers on player tooltips, with the ones you follow in your Fish Journal. Pagle works fully without it, and what it brings in shows nothing once it is a day old. Get it at [pagle.app/download](https://pagle.app/download).

## Working on Pagle

The six addons live under `addons/`, the generated content data under `data/` and the specs under `spec/`. The scripts run in WSL Ubuntu or any x86_64 Linux.

```sh
scripts/setup.sh     # install the pinned toolchain into .tools/
scripts/check.sh     # the full gate: format, lint, types, tests
scripts/package.sh   # build the release zip the way the packager does (commit first)
```

On Windows, `scripts/link-addons.ps1` junctions each `addons/*` folder into your `Interface\AddOns` folder, so edits go live after `/reload`; on macOS, `scripts/link-addons.sh` symlinks them (and with `--retail` makes them loadable in the retail client for UI checks, see [docs/agents/in-game-verification.md](docs/agents/in-game-verification.md#linking-the-addons)).

The project's vocabulary is in [CONTEXT.md](CONTEXT.md), its decisions in [docs/adr](docs/adr) and its release notes in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
