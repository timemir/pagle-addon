What Pagle decided

Rules being tested (one policy, all variants)

  1. One Cast, one announcement. Everything a Cast earns merges into one toast, with each moment shown as a stamp. Level and Zone Master news get a banner instead, and a Legend takes over the whole screen.
  2. Ordinary catches never pop up. Only the Angler bar moves, plus the variant's quiet form if enabled.
  3. Nods (★★★, Species new to zone, Uncharted) are silent and short. If something else is on screen, they go straight to the summary.
  4. Toasts (New Species, records, trophies, ranks, Skill Milestones, other Modules) show one at a time. At most 2 wait in line; extras become "+N more".
  5. Interruption budget (a player setting, on by default): every interruption counts, but only low-priority toasts (size records, new junk or treasure) step down to a nod when it's spent. A record shown as a nod still gets a compact ruler.
  6. Banners and Legends always get through, but they still count against the budget.
  7. Combat hides everything and holds new moments. 2 s after combat ends, one "While you were fighting" toast shows them.
  8. Cozy mode: nothing pops up and nothing plays. Moments wait on the compartment button's counter and the summary card.
  9. Session summary after 5 min without a Cast (placeholder until Stats defines a session).
  10. Rebuilds never re-fire: the Ledger's high-water set (ADR 0002/0004).
