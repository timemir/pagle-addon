The sequence

From the moment Open it is pressed, it takes 9.7 seconds to land. The good shows at about 11.5 seconds for Common and 12.4 for Legendary. A click anywhere, or Esc, skips straight to the reveal. With motion off, the reveal shows at once.

  1. 1.0 s

    The crate drops in

    The case darkens, the purse counts down, and the crate falls from above and squashes on landing. Dust puffs out, and the plaque and the odds appear. Sound: a thud.

  2. 2.4 s

    Seal cracks, lid off

    Three knocks, each harder, and the wax seal glows warmer each time. Then the seal splits, the straps snap and the lid flies off. Light pours out, straw sprays, and the reel opens out of the light. Sound: knocks, a crack, snaps, a whoosh.

  3. 3.3–9.7 s

    The spin

    Cards run right to left in a 6.4-second ease-out. Each card shows only a tier and a kind: an emblem for toast styles, bindings or sound sets, struck in the tier's colour. The light under the line takes the colour of the card passing. Sound: a tick per card, and the bobber dips.

  4. 9.7 s

    It lands

    The line stops wherever it falls inside the winning card, up to 60 px off centre. The other cards dim. By now the player knows the tier and the kind, but not the good. Sound: a soft chime.

  5. 10.4 s

    The card lifts out

    The card rises out of the reel to the centre at 1.5 times its size, still showing its emblem, and the reel drops away.

  6. 10.4–11.6 s

    The wait

    The card holds a moment, longer for higher tiers. From Epic up it trembles and its light swells.

  7. 11.5–12.4 s

    It turns over

    The card turns over to the good itself: half a turn up to Rare, one and a half from Epic. The tier's effects fire as the face comes round. Then the name, the YOURS stamp, and Use it now or Back to the shop. Sound: a whir per half turn, then the tier's chord.

  8. Curio

    A gold card lands

    A crate-only good shows on the reel, and when it lifts out, as a gilded question mark. Its kind stays hidden too.

  9. Curio

    It turns over

    Two and a half turns, then the good. Coins burst out and the gold light holds. Sound: a bell and coins.

Tiers, odds and what each reveal adds

The six tiers, plus gold for goods only the crate holds. The odds are a starting point, not a proposal, and the tier each good gets in this prototype is a stand-in too.

Curio0.5%A gold card with a question mark lands. It spins two and a half turns and turns over to show the good, with a bell and a shower of coins. Only the crate holds these goods.

How the draw works

  • Tier first, then a good. The crate rolls a tier by its odds, then picks a good of that tier the player doesn't own. It never gives a good twice.
  • The result is fixed before anything moves, by the log hash of the crate's entry, as now. The animation only shows it, so a reload mid-spin goes straight to the reveal.
  • The reel shows tier and kind, never the good. Each card is an emblem for its kind in its tier's colour. A good added to the shop later needs nothing for the crate; a new kind of good needs one emblem.
  • The reel is honest. Every other card is rolled the same way, from goods the player could still win, so its kinds and tiers are true to the pool. Near misses are never placed.
  • The odds are on the crate. They stay under the reel for the whole spin.

For you to decide

  • A tier with nothing left. Here that tier drops out and the other tiers' odds are scaled up to fill the gap. Another option is to fall back to the next tier down. The spec needs one rule.
  • Pity. Main's crate has a pity count (CRATE_PITY = 8) built for its four-good pool. With the whole shop in the pool it needs restating: what it guarantees, and whether the reel shows it.
  • Owning everything. The crate could disappear, or show as sold out.
  • Length. About 13 seconds to the buttons suits a first crate but is long for a tenth. Skip covers it, or later crates could spin shorter.

In the addon

  • Motion is only translate, rotate, scale and alpha, plus additive light: the transforms the toast renderer already plays (ANN-R82). Nothing uses skew or blur.
  • The reel is a horizontal ScrollFrame driven from OnUpdate, with about 60 pooled card frames. A card is one emblem texture per kind, tinted with the tier's colour (SetVertexColor), so the art is three textures plus the gold card.
  • The reveal uses the good's own picture, which the shop already shows.
  • The turn-over squashes the card's width and swaps faces at zero. It is not a 3D turn.
  • Sound here is made in the browser. In the addon it would be the client's own sound kits through PlaySound, as the shop uses now. The IDs still have to be chosen and checked in the client.
  • It replaces the current 1.1-second opening in the shop's Motion table.
