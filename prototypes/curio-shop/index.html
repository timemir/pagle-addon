What should the Curio Shop look like and how should it behave? Flip the three shops (bottom bar, ←/→). Open it from the compartment button on the minimap or with /pagle shop. Buy something, equip it, then switch a Module off and see where its goods go. Every variant reads the same wallet.

What to decide

  1. Which shop, or which parts of which ("C's try-on with B's list").
  2. Where equipping happens: Settings only (A), the shop and Settings (B), or the shop is the dressing room and Settings mirrors it (C)?
  3. Buying: one confirm per look (A, B) or one checkout for several (C)? Should buying offer "use it now"?
  4. Income by source: its own tab (A), a stacked bar in the header (B) or a purse column (C)? Show "Spent" at all?
  5. Held at 0: stay silent, or one quiet line? (Pick the "Held at 0" Angler.)
  6. A disabled Module's owned goods: greyed in the shop, a "put away" group, or only a footnote?
  7. Level-earned bindings: show them in the shop as "Angler Level 30" (not for sale), or keep the shop to goods for sale?
  8. Compartment menu: show the balance beside "Curio Shop"?
